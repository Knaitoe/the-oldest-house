# Johnny at his lamp

Each reader's new hallway deals grow more likely to lead to Johnny's halls after five gray hall crossings without a close sighting. The boost has a cap. It resets on a nearby unobstructed encounter or when the reader speaks to him. The short gap between his halls and the single shared Johnny both still apply.

A hungry reader talking to Johnny during a helpful lamp encounter can accept bread, coffee from his thermos, or neither. Johnny gives it freely, then the existing conversation continues. An accepted offer records what the reader took.
