# The Ones Already Inside 3: the people; and five of the owner's notes (0.4.602)

The plan's third slice (`docs/THE_ONES_ALREADY_INSIDE_PLAN.md`): "The four: Rae, Dan, Vera and Daniel painted and standing, talking in
the panel; the failed bodies drawn." The owner's order, 12 November: "start by finishing each unfinished vignette". The lodging house
of 0.4.549 and 0.4.550 had its rooms and nobody in them. Now everybody is where the script's first scene has them. Nothing happens
yet, and the House still does not deal the house.

New builds only. No layout, protocol or Witness change.

## Who is there, and where

- **Rae**, in the middle of the front hall in the coat she came in, turned to the front door, her hands together and working at each
  other (N1: "Rae is in the hall when the first reader comes in").
- **Dan**, on the hall bench in his coat, at the end away from the door, his right hand in his coat pocket. (The other end of the
  bench is where each reader's account will wait.)
- **Vera**, upstairs in the workroom at the table of preparations, folding cloths. She comes down the stair in N2; until that is
  built, a reader who goes up finds her at her work.
- **Daniel**, in his room behind his bolted door, held standing in the frame in its back wall: his back to its plaster, his elbows
  down and his wrists up at the two straps that hang either side of it, his head hanging until he speaks.
- **Three failed bodies.** One sits on the cellar floor at the foot of the dead post, its back to it, its arms up behind its head and
  its wrists roped to the post (N9). One stands in the upper east passage with a hand flat on a guest room's hatch, its head tipped to
  it. One stands in the ground floor's west passage at the jib door, its fingers hooked round the door's frame (N4: the first comes
  through from the passages).

Rae, Dan, Vera and Daniel turn their heads to a reader within eight blocks whom they can see, as far as a head turns, and not their
bodies. Daniel cannot see through his door, so he does not turn to a reader in the passage. The failed bodies turn to nobody.

## How they look

All seven are painted by the cast's one painter (`tools/paint_character_skins.py`), two pixels to the model unit.

- **Rae:** dark hair tied back, a brown wool coat over dark trousers.
- **Dan:** brown hair cut and parted, clean-shaven, a blue-grey coat.
- **Daniel:** the same man: the same hair, gone long, and the same eyes; a full beard; the skin of somebody who has not been out;
  shadows under the eyes, the brows drawn down and the cheeks gone in; a shirt washed grey.
- **Vera:** grey hair pinned up, a dark dress to the ankle under a pale apron.
- **The failed bodies:** what is left of a suit, a faded dress, a nightshirt. Skin gone the colour of candle wax. Their faces are no
  longer kept up: no brows, the lids half down over dull eyes that do not agree (one sits a pixel lower than the other), the mouth a
  thin line a little off the middle, a seam along the jaw. The one in the dress is a little short and the one in the nightshirt a
  little long (the proposal: "wrong proportions beneath their clothes"). The one at the post has rope round its wrists.

## What a hand gets

A hand on one of the four opens the conversation panel, and what they say is also over their head for anybody near. Speech is never
chat. The lines are the neutral script's, as the plan has them (N1 for Rae and Dan, N2's opening for Vera, N3 for Daniel).

- **Rae** makes her request of each reader for themselves, and Dan answers it as the script has him. The five answers are the
  script's: "I'll stay with him." / "What's wrong with him?" / "What is this place?" / "I'd rather see this Vera first." / "It isn't my
  business." Asked what is wrong, Rae has Dan show his hand: he takes it out of his pocket, holds it out for a few seconds, and puts it
  back. A question asked is not offered again. A reader who has given her one of the three answers is not asked again.
- **Dan**, asked for himself, says he is fine, and that he is not. He can be asked what is wrong ("I've stopped sleeping. I don't like
  what my hands do when I'm not watching them.") and what the place is, or left be. He has that say once to a reader, whichever of the
  two the reader went to first: one who has heard it in Rae's request and then turns to him is not told again that he is fine; he
  looks up, and what is left to ask him is offered.
- **Nobody says a line aloud twice running.** For twelve seconds after somebody has said a line aloud they do not say it aloud again.
  A second reader who asks the same thing straight after the first has the words in their own panel.
- **Vera** says the new guests' rooms are ready, and that nobody touches anything in the house that is not theirs. There is nothing
  to answer.
- **Daniel** tells the reader to listen, that there is a key, and to get him out of the frame before she brings Dan down. The five
  answers are the script's: "Dan's here." / "Who are you?" / "I'll get you out." / "I'll come back." / say nothing. He is "The man in
  the frame" over his lines until he has given a reader his name; from then on, for that reader, he is Daniel.
- **A failed body** goes on with what it is doing, and the reader is told what that is, in the grey of the house's other lines.

**What a reader answers is in that reader's own record and binds only them** (the plan: "one reader's yes is never the party's"): whether
Rae has asked them, what they told her, what they asked, whether they promised to stay with Dan, whether they have Daniel's name, what
they told him, whether they promised to get him out. A promise to get Daniel out stays made whatever the reader says to him afterwards.

**Nothing acts on an answer yet.** Rae does not go up the stair, Vera does not come down, nobody is let out, and no room has a name
on its door. Those are the next slices (N1 and N2 in play, then N3).

## The owner's notes on the Pell house, 15 November

Five notes from play, fixed in this release.

- **"white flecks on a portion of june's hand? unsure why that is."** June's right hand has "paint at the quick of two fingernails that
  never quite comes off" (the proposal, 0.4.524): the white line she paints round the lot. It was two lone near-white pixels at the
  bottom edge of her hand, and read as flecks. It is a faint chalky patch worked into two fingertips now, the paint sunk into the skin.
  Her robe and swimsuit skins had the same two pixels. (`tools/paint_character_skins.py`, `june_paint`.)
- **"texture needs to be fixed"** (the garage's roller door). Each of its six blocks drew the whole door, one-pixel stripes and a
  handle, so it read as six tiled panels with six handles. It is one door now: ribs four pixels deep (a lit edge, the face, the shadow)
  that run on from block to block, a bottom rail along its foot, and one handle, on the middle of the lower row. The roller has three
  stages (plain, rail, handle) and the garage sets each block's. (`tools/draw_pell_house.py`, `PellSite`.)
- **"slight mishappen texture"** (the freezer's cord). The cord out of the power point stopped three pixels short of the next block's
  and was stretched to do it, so the line broke and jumped where they met. It runs to the block's edge now, a pixel to a pixel.
  (`tools/draw_pell_things.py`, `power_0` and `power_1`.)
- **"maybe remove her hat at bedtime?"** Tilly's sunhat is on her skin's hat layer; it comes off while June brushes her hair for bed and
  while she sleeps. (`LiteraryRenderers`.)
- **"it doesn't seem week 2 of the pell house began when re-entering its door."** The house played a visit again only once it had stood
  empty for five seconds (`PellVisit.EMPTY`), so a reader who had had all of the first visit, went out through the House's door and came
  straight back, as the house tells them to, walked into the visit they had finished. Now a return that is due a later visit, with nobody
  there still on the earlier one, starts the house afresh (`PellHouse.arrive`). The case that took two readers from Gail's call into the
  week used to start the house afresh itself; it now walks them back in through the arrival, so it fails without the fix.

Not covered: none of the five has been looked at in a client frame; the hat is hidden by pose, so Tilly lying in bed in any other pose
keeps it.

## Two Johnnys in the kitchen (run 1642)

The build job of run 1642 (0.4.601) failed one case, `theKitchensVisitors` (0.4.600): from the sixteenth tick a second Johnny stood in
the familiar kitchen with the one the case had brought in ("role 14 phase 100 is there twice"). It had passed in run 1640; what came

The kitchen now puts away any visitor's body in and round it that is not the one it keeps, when it stands empty and before it makes
anyone new (`KitchenVisitors.strays`). Run here: the cast namespace whole.
