# Harrigan's chair and feeding strays — 0.4.18

Mr. Harrigan's study actor previously stood above his stair chair in the default armor-stand pose. Both living and dead study visits now use synchronized seated leg and arm poses, a seat-height hip, the original northward facing and disabled gravity. The dead visit keeps the bowed head. Existing saved study actors are repaired on their own server tick, preserving UUID, suit, phones, reading and ticket state, funeral state and personal evidence. Bodies already in the casket are left there. A reader can use the separate reading chair, while Harrigan's chair remains occupied.

Lost House cats and wolves tame from one piece of vanilla meat in either hand, with guaranteed native ownership and the existing Follow, Stay, Track deeper, Find way out and Pet controls. Accepted foods are raw and cooked beef, porkchop, chicken, mutton and rabbit; raw and cooked cod and salmon; tropical fish, pufferfish and rotten flesh. A survival or adventure feed consumes exactly one item; creative feeding retains it. The new companion follows immediately, keeps its original UUID and stores ownership and selected orders through native entity saves. Native bone and fish interactions also remain available.

This adoption route applies to living untamed animals spawned as House strays inside the House dimension, within four blocks. It excludes another stored owner, the Mother's kept displays, mirror projections, Hillary's authored opening actor and spectators. Refused feeding consumes nothing. Ordinary vanilla animals keep their existing behavior.

The package version is 0.4.18; layout version remains 17 because the correction does not rebuild rooms. The domestic approach and depth pacing introduced in 0.4.17 remain. No new story resolution is added: Witness still requires seven of nine personal resolutions across at least two kinds, and the three existing endings remain available.

Four required native regression tests cover the old actor's automatic repair while a reader is present, pose and identity persistence, untouched phone props and reading state, both study visits and casket restaging, every accepted meat on cats and wolves through the registered interaction, native owner/order saves, rejected ownership/custody/projection and out-of-House interactions, off-hand feeding and creative inventory behavior. The full build and server GameTest suite are the release gates. A Minecraft client visual walkthrough remains a separate manual check.
