# Bike night 30: more than six (0.4.513)

The proposal (`docs/BIKE_NIGHT_PROPOSAL.md`, "The shape"): "**More than six readers** get a town of their own (the
scene-copy system)."

There are six bikes. Until now a seventh reader came into the same town and found none: "Yours is gone. These are
other people's." They could ride only on the Hopper's pegs.

## Six to a town

Bike night has copies now (`SceneInstances`), as the House's private scenes have, but it is not private: a town is not
one reader's but six readers', one to a bike (`SceneInstances.crowd`, `BikeNight.RIDERS`).

- A reader goes into the first town that stands and has room. So friends go into the same one, as before, and follow
  each other in through a held door as before.
- A reader goes back into the town they keep while it has room: somebody who steps out to the House and comes back is
  with the same people.
- The seventh goes into the next town. It is a second Calder Falls, a million blocks west of the first as every copy
  is, built by the town's own builder and with a night of its own: its own six bikes in its own rack, its own Gus, its
  own three.
- The next town is made ready ahead, when a town is down to its last place and no other has room, so the seventh seldom
  waits. If it is not standing yet, their door waits, as any door onto a copy being made does.
- The server's `sceneCopies` setting is the most towns there can be, as for every scene with copies.

Everything that is "in every copy" already was: the stones at the end of the row are stood in each town by its own
night, the lane's door is each town's own way on, and leaving by either door finishes the night whichever town it was.

## The case

One new case, 101 in the Bike night namespace. It builds a real second town.

- A town has copies and is nobody's own: friends follow each other into it.
- Four readers: each goes into the first town by its own door, and keeps it. No other town is begun.
- The fifth: the next town is being made ready. The sixth: the first town.
- The seventh, out in the House: their door waits while their town is made.
- The second town stands: a million blocks west, at the same height. The seventh's door opens on it, and it is theirs
  to come back to. It has its own way on, in its own lane's wall.
- Its park and street are the first's, block for block, but for what a night has moved in the first.
- Nothing has begun in it. The seventh goes in: a night of its own begins there, with all six bikes in its own rack,
  and the first town still has its own.
- One of the six goes out. An eighth reader takes the place left in the first town; the seventh keeps the second; the
  one who went out comes back to the first.

That the town's builder runs under the House's copy builder at all (recorded, and laid in slices at a shifted place)
had not been tried before. It does.

## Left with the owner

- **A reader who comes in after the chase has begun** still comes into the same town, as before: the park is empty,
  the gate is open, and whatever bikes are left are in the rack. Only a full town sends somebody on. If latecomers
  should have a town of their own instead, it is one more condition.
- **Friends are not kept together across towns.** Seven who come to the door together are six and one.
- A second town is a second whole town. On a server where five readers are in one at once, the House builds one.
