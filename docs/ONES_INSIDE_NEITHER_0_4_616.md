Both die, in two rooms or one. Vera starts putting the room back before anybody has moved. What happens to her and the house after

## Who

A reader knows the link once they have seen the cut come up on both men (0.4.606), or had the truth out of Vera (0.4.609). Anyone
else's blow on either man does what it did before: nothing. No blow counts once Dan has been put back, Daniel completed or the house
severed, or while any of those is under way; once both are dead, none of them can begin.

## Blows

- A blow from a reader who knows lands on both men wherever the other is: both double over and are heard (Daniel strains in his
  straps). Dan asks the striker to stop after the first; at the third, Daniel asks who is doing it, heard near him.
- Six blows from anyone who knows, at most one from each hand every half second, and both die where they are. Strapped, Daniel hangs
  dead in the straps; free, he lies where he fell. Every reader in the house is told; the line is one for one room and one for two.
- The dead are kept where they fell and turn to nobody.

## After

- Vera, heard through the house, says two places will want feeding, comes down by the real doors and lays them at the dining table,
  one at a time. A blow on her while she is on her way or laying stops her for good; she lays no more and Rae does not come.
- With both laid, Rae comes in, knocks a chair over, and asks which place it was. Readers near the table get the last image.
- A reader can tell Rae what Vera did ("Vera did this. All of it.").
- A reader who knows the third way can still work the ratchet and the catch (0.4.615) after the deaths: the house is let go under
  Vera, who sits down on the floor. Daniel's straps and the failed bodies are left as they are.
- Doing none of these is leaving her the house; the account says so.
