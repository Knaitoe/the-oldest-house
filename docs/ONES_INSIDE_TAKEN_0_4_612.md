# The Ones Already Inside 12: taken into the walls; Vera's one activity (0.4.612)

The plan's twelfth slice (`docs/THE_ONES_ALREADY_INSIDE_PLAN.md`): "N12: taken readers in the passages; Vera using the doubles." The
House still does not deal the house.

New builds only. No layout, protocol or Witness change. The logic is `LodgingTaken`. The reader's unfinished body gains three standing
poses (STAND, LED, STEADY) in `client.LodgingRenderers`.

## The disturbance

- **When.** Eight seconds of the house's clock after the first attempt is done (landed or stopped), with the first intrusion over.
- **Where.** The failed body at the jib door comes through again and walks to the room where the two men were put together: the
  parlor (through the hall and the parlor door) or Daniel's room (along the back passage and through his door). Readers on the ground
  floor are told the jib door banged open.
- **Whom.** Whoever is nearest it in that room: a reader, or Rae, or Dan, or Daniel if he is out of the frame. Never Vera. Rae, Dan or
  Daniel cries out as it takes hold, and pulls against it (STRAIN); readers who see it are told.
- **Out.** It walks them backwards through the house by the arm, slower than it came, and lets go of them in the west passage behind
  the jib door. If anyone is still in that room and it has not been driven off, it goes back for a second (two at most). Then the jib
  door swings to and its latch drops on the house's side.
- **Stopping it.** Two blows from somebody else, or three from the one held, break its grip, and it goes back empty-handed. Five blows
  in all drive it off for good. A blow stuns it for a moment and puts it back half a pace.

## In the passages

- **Knocking.** A hand on the lath from inside the wall knocks. Readers on the other side within nine blocks hear it; Rae and Dan say
  so once each. If the body is in a room with nobody in its hand, it lets the room be and goes back toward the knocking; if it is
  holding somebody, it stops for a moment. This is the taken reader's way to help.
- **A hatch.** Sliding a hatch back from inside the wall is seen from the room.
- **The latch.** From inside, a standing hand finds only the gap at the jamb; four crouched tries work the latch up. From the house's
  side a hand lifts it at once.
- **Rae, Dan or Daniel in the wall.** They stay where it left them while it is about. Once it is over, they come out as soon as the
  latch is up, or work it themselves after forty-five seconds, and walk back to where they belong.

## Vera's one activity

Four seconds after the jib door is shut, with Vera back in Daniel's room, she reads the readers present: those who answered her offer
"I'll help you" are for her; "Send Dan back" and "No" are against. Those who never answered do not count; with a tie she waits.

- **More for her.** She goes up to the room of one who is for her (first of them who has a body in their bed), draws back the
  blanket, and brings the body down by the wrist. It walks where she walked, a step behind. At the frame it stands with its hands at
  its sides. When its original is near she asks them to put its hands where theirs would go. Only that reader can answer: "Show me
  where." puts its hands on the frame (STEADY); "Not with my hands." leaves it standing. Anybody else's hand on Vera gets nothing.
- **More against her.** She goes up to the workroom and calls down the stair, heard by everybody in the house. A reader who was
  against her and comes up can promise to come down and hold Daniel when she calls, or refuse.

Nothing is done to anybody's body except by that reader's own reply. What a promise or a steadied body leads to belongs to the endings.
