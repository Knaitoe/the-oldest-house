# The line (0.4.212)

The fourth of the dark halls' horrors (docs/DARK_HALL_HORRORS_PROPOSAL.md), after Exploration #4 in the book. The owner, 1
November: these "should be the most extreme yet, either psychologically or physically".

Slot 142, layout 68, no protocol change. One new Witness story: **65 sources / 49 required / two kinds / three endings**.

## Where it is dealt

Only in the dark band, from depth sixteen (`DarkHalls.DARK_DEPTH`): one door among several, with the usual story spacing
(`DarkHalls.horror`). It is a one-shot room; a failed visit can be dealt again.

## The space

Built whole in the dark halls' ash (`LineSite`), with no light at all:

- **The hall:** a short dark hall leads to a doorframe.
- **The space:** past the frame is a space with no walls a reader can find in the dark: forty-nine wide, eighty deep and six
  high, with two-by-two columns standing about in it.
- **The line:** tied to the doorframe at hand height, it winds between the columns for about ninety blocks, never within three
  blocks of a column, to an explorer's pack at the far end.
- **The pack:** a tape recorder lies on it (the account). The way on is in the far wall behind it.
- **The tag:** a luggage tag knotted onto the line at the frame (the source).

## What happens

**The line.**
- Read the tag, then take it again to take hold of the line.
- While held, the line shows a little within arm's length of the hand on it, and nowhere else, and it leads.
- More than a block and a half off the line, or running, the hand loses it, and the dark is total (blindness) until the reader
  walks back to it. The hand finds it again, unless they are still running.

**The tugs.** A third of the way along, it tugs: hard, from behind, once; then again three seconds later; then steadily.

**What has hold of it.** Something has picked up the line behind the reader, and it comes along it toward the nearest hand on it.
- It comes faster while that hand stands still.
- It is never seen. It is heard: footsteps in the dark along the line, the line creaking under its weight.
- Its weight drags at every hand on the line.
- Within four blocks it drags the reader back across the floor toward it, scraping: half a heart every second, never below two
  hearts. The view dips and the hands slip.
- The cue comes first: "Something walking along the line, near."

**Letting go** shakes it off, and leaves the reader blind until they find the line again.

Holding on is the only way to the end, and holding on is how it reels them in.

## How it ends

**Reeled all the way.** The line goes slack, and the floor isn't there. The reader comes to three doors shallower than they were
(real depth taken off their return stack), and:
- the line is wound round their arm, an item that will not come off or be dropped until they next sleep;
- one ordinary stack they carried is gone. It is found the next time they reach the pack, tied to its strap with another length of
  the same line.

The visit can be tried again.

**Reaching the pack first:**
- the line goes slack behind the reader;
- whatever had hold of it has let go;
- the tape recorder on the pack has the account (`held_on`).

The Witness line: "I held on." Kind: survival.

## Co-op

The line and the thing on it are shared; what each reader holds, loses and finds is their own.

- Every hand on the line feels the tugs.
- A reader holding the line at the door can pull twice on the tag to warn whoever is out there.
- It goes for the nearest hand on the line. If the one ahead lets go, that may be the one at the door.

## Literature

**The source:** the tag's "RULES FOR THE LINE", in pencil.
- Tie off at the door; always one hand on it.
- If you lose it, come back the way you came: it will be where you left it.
- Don't run.
- "If it pulls, it's me."
- On the back, pressed harder: "If it pulls twice, it isn't. If it pulls and doesn't stop, let go."

**The account:** "The account: the tape". A transcript of the cassette, pencilled inside the pack's flap in the reader's own hand.
- Every entry is dated days after the day they found it.
- The entries describe this room: the columns; the times they lost the line; the tugs; being dragged; being reeled in and what was
  gone; walking without stopping.
- The last entry is the reader finding the pack, picking up the recorder, and knowing whose voice is on it.

**Elsewhere:**
- the co-op line;
- the miniature (a cigar box painted black inside, one cotton thread across it to a pea-sized rucksack);
- the staircase prose ("the dark with the line through it");
- the co-author pool;
- the advancement under survival.

**Limits held:**
- nothing gory is shown;
- the thing is never seen;
- the reader's gender, colour and age are never named;
- lines are on the action bar, never in chat.

## Tests

Five `LineTests` in the literary namespace:
- dealt only in the dark band, with the tag, a line clear of every column, the space and the pack;
- holding the line leads, and letting go is total dark until it is found again, and nobody can run and keep it;
- a third of the way along it tugs, then comes along the line, faster for standing still, and drags without taking the last two
  hearts;
- reeled all the way, the reader loses depth, the line is wound round their arm until they sleep, and something of theirs is gone;
- reaching the pack first, the line goes slack, what was taken is tied to the strap, and the tape is in the reader's own hand.
