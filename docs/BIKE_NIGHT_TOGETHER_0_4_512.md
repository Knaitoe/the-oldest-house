# Bike night 29: who rode with whom (0.4.512)

The proposal (`docs/BIKE_NIGHT_PROPOSAL.md`, "Fit with the House"): "accounts are each reader's own, with co-op lines
for who rode with whom and who carried whom."

The account said only "For some of it I had somebody on the pegs behind me" and "For some of it I was on the pegs of
somebody else's bike." It named nobody, and said nothing of who else was there.

## Who rode with whom

When Tully says go, each reader in the park has it in their record who else was there (`BikeNight.together`). Their
account says so, before the streets: "Sam rode with me.", or "Sam and Kim rode with me.", or for more, "Sam, Kim and
Lee rode with me." Somebody alone has no such line.

## Who carried whom

When one reader is on another's pegs, each has the other's name in their record, once however often it happens. Their
accounts: "For some of it I had Sam on the pegs behind me." and "For some of it I was on the pegs behind Kim." A record
from before this, with no name in it, says "somebody" as it did.

The names are the readers' names as the game has them.

## The case

One new case, 100 in the Bike night namespace. Two readers in the park when the three come and Tully says go: each
has the other, and their accounts say so. The second on the first's pegs, twice: each has who, once, and their
accounts say who. Three names are listed as three. A record with no names in it says somebody.

## Left with the owner

- Who rode with whom is who was in the park when the chase was called. Somebody who comes into the town after that is
  not in anybody's line, and has nobody in theirs.
- Nothing is said of who was caught, or of who did not make it through the Hollow. An account is its reader's own.
