## The night

300 ticks into the hold, the night begins (`NIGHT`, phase 6).

- **Jude** walks in from the back step and leans on the kitchen counter. While his cut is undressed he holds his side.
- **Casey** follows him in 40 ticks later and stays by him.
- **Nina** is in the living room after a wounding, or after a watched shed. After a witnessed shed she is gone.
- **Len** says he is going down to the boat and tells everyone to lock their doors. He walks off the porch, round the east side of the cabin and down to the boathouse. He goes in and shuts the iron door behind him. From then on he is only reached through it.

## The kitchen

> *"A player can provide first aid while another checks the costume plan with Casey. Jude's account is interrupted by pain and by Casey trying to minimize what happened. The responsible player can insist that Casey stop talking over him. Another role can do exactly the same work."*

While Jude is hurt, or saw who it was, and Casey is within eight blocks of him, Jude gets as far as "I was getting out of the—" and Casey talks over him. Any reader can say "Casey. Let him talk." After that Casey keeps quiet for everyone.

Then Jude can be asked, once each per reader:

- **Who came into the shed?** Somebody small in a coat and gloves, who went for his bag. If the shed was witnessed: Nina, who didn't look surprised. If it was watched: someone behind the shed, a long time; Len, maybe.
- **Do you tie the mask on, or buckle it?** Only for a reader who found or carries the elastic strap. He ties his; the strap behind the shed has a buckle at each end, cut to fit somebody else.
- **Who knew you'd change in the shed?** Casey, and whoever Casey told. If Casey is in the room, he admits he told Nina.
- **Did you see anyone by the boathouse today?** Nina, trying the door, before they ate.

The first-aid bag or the bathroom kit still dresses his cut.

## The dock

A witnessed shed leaves its back window broken, and running prints along the beach toward the boathouse. A watched shed leaves boot prints in the needles behind it, where someone stood a long time.

The camera's flash records any of these in front of it, loosely: a twelve-block reach and a wide cone.

## The boathouse

> *"Len wants a locked door between himself and the attacker. He also wants nobody opening the safe."*

The boathouse now has its inside: the safe in the far corner, a chair, a bench and a lantern. The safe can be seen through the front windows.

Knocking on the iron door (right-clicking it) brings Len to the other side of it. He says go back, lock up, and nobody touches anything down there. A reader can:

- ask to be let in (no);
- ask who would want to hurt him (nobody, kids, go back up);
- having seen the prints, say somebody came up out of the water right by there. He says she's been round the back, then that he didn't say she;
- having seen the safe, ask what's in it (nothing that's yours);
- say they'll keep watch. He thanks them, and says don't come in.

Before he is inside, nobody answers.

## The cabin: Casey's plan

> *"Casey's plan lists harmless effects and locations. Those details separate a staged noise from a real approach. Let the party use this knowledge. Destroying the plan in embarrassment is a consequential act."*

Casey's bag on the bedroom floor holds his plan, in his capitals:

1. The breaker.
2. Three knocks at the front window.
3. The back door, rattled, not opened.
4. The shed, a change, back in normal clothes.
5. At midnight, Jude standing on the end of the dock in the mask, a torch on him from the porch, the big finish.

Mask, coverall and rubber knife are in Jude's bag, and the stage blood is for number 5 only. So the figure on the dock was where the plan put Jude, at the wrong hour, while Jude was standing in front of Casey.

What can happen to the plan:

- **Taken.** A reader takes it out of the bag and reads it. Casey asks for it back once ("Give it to him." or "Not yet."). Given back, he takes it to the fire.
- **Burned by a reader.** A reader can put it in the fire themselves.
- **Burned by Casey.** If nobody has taken it 2400 ticks into the night, and Casey hasn't turned, he goes to his bag and puts the plan in the fire. A reader nearby or watching sees it. If someone took it first, he finds the bag empty and says nothing.
- **Handed over.** If Casey has turned, he tells whoever asks where the plan is.

## The player set down

> *"The wildcard's earlier object can contradict Nina's statement about never going outside."*

After a wounding, Nina's way back in reaches the back door 160 ticks after the splash: "The back door again, slowly. Someone breathing hard. Water dripping on the boards." She says she was in the bathroom.

## Telling Casey

> *"Separate private conversations can occur, with explicit ways to tell another NPC what was learned."*

A reader can tell Casey each thing they know that he doesn't:

- the wet prints;
- the blood on the dock;
- the plan's midnight scare, against the figure;
- the strap;
- the costume in the boat;
- the tape.

He has an answer for each. After two (or one, after a witnessed shed), he stops calling it a bit: "I didn't hire anyone else. I swear to you." Everyone near him hears it, he stops minimising, he won't burn the plan, and he tells anyone who asks where it is.

Nina, asked, says she didn't go outside and was in the bathroom. Told about the wet prints or the tape, she says somebody else went swimming. Told what Jude saw, she says she was looking for the toilet.
