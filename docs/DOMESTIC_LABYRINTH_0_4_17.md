# The domestic approach — 0.4.17

The labyrinth should have time to feel like somewhere people once lived. Its entrance continues the manor's timber and ordinary warm lighting. Cupboard fronts and mismatched wall finishes interrupt the long approach; the first landing has floorboards, a wooden bench, shoe rack and a window backed by plaster. Tom's finite supplies remain where they were.

Straight, bent and crossing corridors contain pieces of other homes: tiled kitchen counters, a laundry nook with folded cloth, a bedroom with a bed and bedside cupboard, and a dining table with two chairs. The finishes stop at the corridor walls. They are open physical recesses, reachable on foot while all registered side doors remain reachable. Smaller furnished recesses survive in the dead-end walls of every gray maze, including the deepest. They leave the existing maze floor and fold sleeves clear. These are scenery, not additional stories or Witness resolutions. The bedroom fragment follows the labyrinth's existing no-sleep/no-spawn rules.

## Pacing

Depth is the number of crossings in the player's saved return route from the hallway root. Walking back reduces it. Shared completed stories cannot bypass the approach for a new explorer.

| Depth | What becomes possible |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Domestic landing, familiar corridors and quiet sitting room. The plain physical maze has a small weight from depth 4. No automatic story doors or false sensory leaks. |
| 6–7 | Stories start at 12% per eligible dealing, rising 20 points with each eligible dry dealing. Flooded passage, false distance, camp and long hallway enter the first deeper tier. Impossible geometry still has zero discovery chance. |
| 8–9 | First folded maze; one 3% anomaly roll shared by all doors at an arrival. Gray maze lighting starts thinning, portable lights can shift, ambient Mother visits and natural Growl scheduling become possible. |
| 10–11 | Stories use the established 30% base, rising 25 points per dry dealing. Spiral stair, fractured route, Light Sink and moving threshold enter the second tier. |
| 12–13 | Larger folded maze; 5% anomaly budget. Lighting thins further. |
| 14–15 | Hotel loop, compression, gravity and duplicate passage enter the third tier. |
| 16+ | Abyss maze; 8% anomaly budget, reaching 10% at depth 24. Ordinary maze fixtures can disappear completely, although household remnants remain. |

Three later visits still separate impossible discoveries. Rest-room spacing and finite camp supplies remain. Quiet crossings do not accrue dry-deal credit. Stored legacy dry streaks cannot bypass the depth gate. The Growl starts with its full fifteen-minute mean at depth eight, stays far away through nine, gains below-floor origins from ten, and near origins from sixteen. Its randomized gaps, minimum interval and deferred unseen changes remain.

An explicit vignette scent given to Hillary can still deal a story on the next dealing. Living-pet custody retains its 65% base and strong owner-specific Mother weight even in the approach. These exceptions preserve deliberate tracking and urgent rescue. Operator fixture commands still reach rooms directly.

## Existing worlds

Layout version 17 decorates the existing version-16 landing, familiar halls and mazes in place. Their old coordinates, doors and maze seeds remain. Caches are not refilled, ordinary placed lights and dropped items remain, and pets are not replaced. A saved per-place checkpoint prevents a restart during the short upgrade from redressing a finished room. Active vignettes, stored personal evidence, Mother's custody and completed endings are retained. Older structural migrations keep their existing scope; a forced operator rebuild remains a rebuild.

Witness still has **nine eligible stories**, requires **seven distinct personal resolutions across at least two kinds**, and exposes **three endings**. Household fragments deliberately remain outside that pool.
