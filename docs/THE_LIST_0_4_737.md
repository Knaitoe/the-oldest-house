# The list, stand-ins 3: the town out with flashlights (0.4.737)

The third of the stand-ins `docs/THE_LIST_PLAN.md` lists, and the plan's HOME DANGER read. No protocol, layout or Witness change.

Part 6b (`docs/THE_LIST_0_4_724.md`) left the townspeople out with the deputy for later, as they needed figures of their own. They are
out now.

- On night three and night four, while the hunt is on, three of the town's people come out from beside the substation, each with a
  flashlight lit in their hand: Mr. Purcell, a man from the feed store in a work jacket; Ronnie, younger, in a cap and flannel; and Mrs.
  Teague in a barn coat. Each walks streets of their own, out and back: Main Street's far lane; Avenue K down to Ninth and along it;
  Avenue K down to Seventh and along it. Each flashlight's beam is a real light four blocks ahead at chest height.
- If one of them sees the one who went in (within sixteen blocks, within fifty degrees of where they face, nothing in the way) they
  stop, keep the light on them and shout over to the others, and the deputy leaves his round and goes there. The one who went in is
  told another flashlight has found them, and the hunt reads them as standing in light while the searcher has them in sight. Lost for
  two seconds, the searcher walks on, and does not shout for them again for fifteen.
- They look for nobody else. Another reader in their way is only seen, and a spectator never.
- **HOME DANGER.** On night three, if the House read the one who went in's home as a danger to them, all three go first to Ninth in
  front of that reader's house and stand there with their lights on it for half a minute, then take their streets. That reader alone
  is told that flashlights have stopped in the street in front of their house. Nothing names what the House read.
- They are never saved: made each night three and night four when the hunt begins, sent home at first light and when the night ends,
  their lights out.

model and painter, and the art check covers the sheets.
