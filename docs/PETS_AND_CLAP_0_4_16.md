# Pets and hide-and-clap — 0.4.16

The companion wheel draws at native GUI resolution without the screen blur. Its centre pets the animal (E or Enter); the four directions retain Follow, Stay, Track deeper and Find way out. Petting produces hearts, a quiet response and reassurance, retaining the movement command. Ownership, six-block reach, custody and repeated-packet checks apply on the server. Native tameable animals, including the mod's cats, Hillary, stray wolves and recovered Pekingese, are supported.

When the House first appears, Hillary requests another pat. A greeting on the previous day does not start this introduction early. She stands and gets attention despite Stay, and the first pat confirms guidance. She waits if her player falls behind. At the porch she restores native ownership and the saved wheel command. Once the player has entered the House, ordinary companion controls resume.

Hide-and-clap keeps the minute from equipping the blindfold. Each actual clap sends a short personal pulse indicating direction and proximity, without revealing the room. The final wardrobe repeats its muffled signal every 3.5 seconds after the initial pair; close cues ask the player to open it. Failure starts with approaching feet; at 0.6 seconds the server removes the bound cloth and exposes the existing textured girl's full figure. The neck twist begins at 1.5 seconds and death follows at 2 seconds. The room stays locked throughout. The original cloth and carried possessions drop normally, including in keepInventory worlds, and failure respawns the player in the manor.

A real vignette death transfers nearby standing, unleashed following companions into the Mother's saved living custody. Pets ordered to Stay, another player's pets, spectators and exterior projections are excluded. The exact original UUID, entity type, owner, health, equipment and attachment/order data are saved; the den's display copy carries neither the original UUID nor its equipment. Peaceful/banished Mother outcomes prevent further capture.

While she holds a living pet, its owner's base vignette opportunity rises from 30% to 65% and her den has eight times its ordinary unvisited weight. This urgent case overrides recent-den suppression, without changing another explorer's odds. Custody clears the bias as soon as the pet is recovered or disposed of.

On arrival she states the demand and starts a 60-second ransom window of time actually spent alive in her den. Leaving or logging out pauses it; restart preserves the remainder. Click the Mother or the pet with a diamond, golden apple, netherite scrap/ingot, four emeralds, enchanted equipment, or a vignette keepsake/artifact (including the phones, scratch ticket and church key). Ordinary renamed rubble and borrowed shelf objects are refused. A successful exchange keeps the real payment and restores the original living animal and its saved wheel order. Multiple lost pets each require a payment. If unpaid, she kills the displayed animal and permanently closes that custody entry, returning to her normal face. The older dead-pet recovery exchange remains separate. Vignette keepsakes can also settle the borrowed-shelf exchange.

For a living payment, sneak-click the Mother or the waiting pet with empty hands. She accepts a reachable animal or villager within four blocks, including untamed strays and the trader's own tame pets; another owner's tame animals, somebody else's leashed animal, den display animals and mirror projections are excluded. The real offering leaves the world and enters sealed saved custody with its UUID and full entity state. It cannot immediately be bought back or reused as a payment.

Another survival player can replace the pet only if physically within her reach and enclosed in the same small closed room. The server flood-fills player-sized space, including vertical exits: an open doorway, missing roof, large open den, player outside the enclosure, creative player or spectator cannot qualify. Bring the player and Mother into the room, close it, then empty-hand sneak-click your waiting pet from outside. This is an explicit exchange; enclosing them alone does not execute it. The offered player suffers a real native death before custody is released. Their identity is recorded without serializing or cloning a player. Living trades also settle ordinary shelf debts, and never grant the peaceful Mother resolution.

These changes affect existing scenes and add no Witness source: **nine eligible stories, seven distinct personal resolutions across at least two kinds, three endings**. Ransom alone does not grant a peaceful Mother resolution.
