## What this review suggests for the mod

These are interpretations from the reviewed appendices and letters, rather than claims to have completed a close reading of every page of the novel.

**Ordinary life supplies the stakes.** The poems preserve affection, travel, jokes and particular people alongside grief. Roots is a promise of shelter. The final Pelafina letter dwells on a parent's ability to comfort a hurt child. That gives familiar spaces and living companions a purpose beyond resting between scares: they are things the explorer can come to care about. It supports the owner's decision to retain the opening wait and the current calm-route work.

**A paper can change how an existing place is understood.** The Panther describes a captive whose history and pain are concealed by the name observers give him. Its placement before the prisoner can invite a second look without deciding the ending for the player. A new lore find does not need another monster, objective or mechanical reveal to matter.

**The reader should be able to revisit evidence.** Pelafina's November 1 letter revises the apparent New Director and her earlier account of writing. The player possesses the earlier pages, so the tension remains available for their own judgment. Keep distinct authors, dates and immutable originals. Avoid a universal narrator that resolves every contradiction or turns a frightened claim into server authority.

**Reading can have its own geography.** The Pelican dedications carry a journey across cities; the cipher makes words themselves something to navigate. Scatter complete pieces, pace one ordered relationship across real travel, and keep careful reading optional. This extends exploration beyond traversing rooms while preserving difficult Witness requirements.

**Clarity and tenderness deepen distress.** Starting Pelafina solely at her most paranoid would lose the relationship the letters reveal. A sequence with affection and later reflection gives the frightening passages context. Future writing and scene revisions can use that range without identifying Pelafina with the mod's Mother or reducing illness to a source of monster behavior.
