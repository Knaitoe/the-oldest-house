# Fire and companions — 0.4.33

Minecraft Java 1.21.1 / NeoForge. Layout 29; protocol 29. Use the same JAR on clients and server. This pass changes the existing staircase and companions; it adds no Witness source. Seventeen sources still require thirteen personal resolutions across two kinds, with three endings.

## House of Leaves

Down the staircase entrance hallway, the lectern on the right carries a small **House of Leaves**. Interacting gives each explorer one five-leaf copy and flint and steel. The supply is saved and finite, including when the inventory is full; a residual original drops for its owner instead of disappearing.

Put the book in the off hand and flint and steel in the main hand. Use the five cold hearths in order on the outer edges of the first five corner landings. Each hearth consumes one leaf and one use of the striker. The last leaf consumes the booklet. The remaining leaf count is visible on its name and first page. If the booklet is lost, ordinary paper can feed the remaining hearths; other collected letters are not accepted as fuel here.

Each personal fire makes the next stretch available. Beyond the next unlit hearth, black terrain fog closes to a short range and the server stops further descent, returning the explorer to a saved safe tread. Full brightness cannot bypass the movement gate. Lighting the fifth hearth makes the full, real 1,280-block staircase descent available. It does not shorten the stairs, teleport to the cell or award ending credit. The equally deep continuation remains. Turning around and using the ordinary entry return remains possible; the cell is still the commitment point.

The hearths are physical campfires shared in the world, but lighting credit is saved separately for each explorer. A peer must feed the same five hearths personally, even when their flames are already visible. Repeating one hearth cannot count twice or consume another leaf. The native book is tied to its reader. Relogging and saving retain the leaf components and fire progression. A once-only dressing checkpoint adds the lectern and hearths after current visitors leave; it does not rebuild the shaft, refill the existing cache or replace original actors or papers. Saved explorers already below the new introduction retain an open route when upgrading.

## Tracking through doors

The saved Follow / Stay / Track deeper / Find way out orders remain on actual native cats and wolves. A scent no longer clears the stored movement order. Hillary's request for a scent also deals an already reached manor threshold rather than waiting indefinitely for a later dealing. Opening the command wheel does not silently erase exit guidance.

Guides use several connected floor tiles ahead, avoiding the one-tile waypoint that lies inside Minecraft navigation's stopping range. A guide lagging behind its owner catches up; a guide already ahead waits for the explorer. Guidance resumes at the new room after the short depth hesitation.

Door transfers capture standing owned companions within 32 blocks, including a guide already ahead at a door. Saved Stay is excluded even if its native sitting flag has not settled yet. Foreign animals, captives, projections, leashed animals and passengers remain excluded. Arrival retains the native UUID, owner, health and movement order, clears stale room/fear presentation and installs guidance on the arriving native entity. Arrival offsets prefer an actual supported floor rather than an empty edge over a drop.

## Petting

Empty-hand interaction opens the wheel. Choose **Pet** at the center, or press **E**. A server-approved pat starts a 44-tick response, with a 48-tick cooldown. Movement pauses briefly without replacing the saved command. Stay remains Stay. Native ownership, range and captivity checks still apply.

The first-person hand reaches out and strokes. The player's native third-person arm and sleeve follow the stroke. Dogs lean and wag their tail; cats tilt and rub their head and curl their tail. Nearby observers receive the same short response. Hearts and the existing purr / dog sounds accompany it. The client response expires or clears at a world change and carries no authority over ownership or progression.
