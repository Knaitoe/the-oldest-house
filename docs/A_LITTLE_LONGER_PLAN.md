# A Little Longer: the build plan

From the owner's proposal (`docs/A_LITTLE_LONGER_PROPOSAL.md`, 2 November 2026). The proposal is the canon; this file only orders
the work into slices, each a release with its own record, and lists the defaults chosen where the proposal leaves something open.
Nothing here edits the proposal.

## Shape

- One private story instance per reader (the scene-copy system), never shared: "A second player's jokes or decisions should not
  speak for the reader's romantic interest."
- Seven chapters, each a resumable visit boundary (the owner's question: "could the chapters register as different visits?" Yes).
  Rowan's state, the shared acts and the cast are the reader's own record.
- No timers drive the story: chapters move on when the reader acts or answers. Ambient behaviour runs on its own.
- The conversation panel (Talk) carries every line; replies are labelled choices; nothing important goes to chat.
- Rowan is one authored person. Casting follows a settled DRAWN_TO read, fixed at first meeting; unknown reads get the companion
  route with the reader able to initiate. AGE only picks an adult variant.
- No affection points. The record keeps explicit acts: what was held, returned, said, accepted, offered.
- The cut is fixed and cannot be prevented, earned, or blamed. Afterwards the room is changed for good.

## Slices

1. **The place.** Shop, flat, roof landing and a short stretch of the House's corridor, built whole with their own materials and
   fittings (workbench, parts trays, the twice-fixed chair, kettle, two cups, toaster, radio, coat hook, rain window, the patch of
   sky). Every door and fitting answers. Not dealt.
2. **Rowan.** Painted (two adult casts), standing, walking real paths, humming at the bench, the conversation panel.
3. **Chapter 1, the workbench.** The panel held, the screw, the lamp; the penciled route; the one remembered act.
4. **Chapter 2, the kettle.** The invitation to stay, the uncracked cup, the comfortable chair, the toast and its joke, Rowan's small
   kindness, reciprocal questions about the present.
5. **Chapter 3, the radio.** The original tune; the disagreement and its repair; the invitation outside (direct, flirting, quiet).
6. **Chapter 4, the roof.** The sky between the wrong buildings; shared silence; the admission; the remembered act recalled; the hand,
   the kiss, at a believable pace; the companionship route.
7. **The return indoors.** "I just don't want you to go yet."
8. **Chapter 5, tomorrow.** The shifted door; Rowan's coat; the breakfast scrap written by two people; the present choice.
9. **Chapter 6, the cut.** The seam: architecture first, then the wall, the counter and Rowan receding; one human reaction; the
   radio stopping mid-phrase. Works from every position; no rescue branch.
10. **Chapter 7, the room afterward.** The changed layout, the cup, the chair, the radio, the breakfast scrap as the keepsake; the
    account; the Witness source; dealing.
11. **Leaving early and resuming.** A shorter evening with nothing invented; reunion on return; nothing undone by a restart.
12. **Personhood.** SELF address, HAIR's one callback, HOME's first question, AGE casting; neutral for every unread state.

## Defaults chosen (any can move)

- Witness: one source of the memory kind, resolved by reading the account in the room afterward.
- Keepsake: the breakfast scrap, its text from the reader's own conversation; one identity, never duplicated.
- The radio tune: an original short melody generated by a tool in `tools/`, as other scenes' audio is.
- The flat's street is an authored backdrop behind glass.
