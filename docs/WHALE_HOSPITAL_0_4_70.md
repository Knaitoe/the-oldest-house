# Whalestoe hospital and letter writing

Version 0.4.70 builds on the 0.4.69 child-arrival and streetlight fix branch. The institute receives sixteen new native 32-pixel hospital materials, twenty ward fittings, enamel rails, hospital stairs and shelving, ribbed glass, painted doors and real 27-slot ward cabinets. Every earlier PNG remains unchanged. The material atlas was generated with the built-in Image Generation tool and exported by `tools/build_whale_hospital_assets.py`; its source is preserved in `tools/asset_sources/whale_hospital_atlas.png`.

Walls vary between intact sage paint, restrained peeling and damp patches near exterior walls. Linoleum, glazed dado, acoustic ceilings, steel and enamel bed frames, vinyl chairs, laminate desks, sinks, cupboards and ceiling lights replace the wooden hospital placeholders. The original numbered pigeonhole fronts and door plates remain. Attic headroom, room locations, door states and the missing seventh plate remain authoritative.

The wooden Thursday sign becomes an open notebook on a bedside table in her room. Its reading is private and follows that reader's correspondence, retaining the crossed-out dates. The opening and attic letters rest on hospital reading tables; the exact native lectern originals move into their new block entities.

Clicking the paper desk offers free writing, three optional prose beginnings and an optional other hand. The native book stays editable and is signed by its actual writer. Starters leave existing prose alone. The other hand appends a page without changing existing pages, filtering or item components, at most three times per writer. These choices consume no additional supplies and grant no resolution credit. The drawer still contains four finite sheets; the outgoing slot still accepts three signed letters in that writer's own name, gives its private earlier-dated replies, and box seven returns the exact posted originals.

Existing institutes receive a separate, once-only in-place repair. It waits for native chunk/entity sections, cameras and living residents. Only recognized earlier materials and fixtures change. Actual barrel contents retain all twenty-seven slots, item components, custom names and loot metadata through saved custody; lectern originals do likewise. Interrupted custody resumes without overwriting an original with an already emptied container. Later removals, depleted drawers, private letters, pet identities and completed endings remain saved. A player-edited calendar sign is retained.

Layout stays 40. Protocol is 44; use matching 0.4.70 clients and server. The pool remains 43 eligible sources, 33 personal resolutions across at least two kinds, and three endings. The resolution is still the writer's returned original read in her room. Optional prose, the notebook and scenery add no source.

Validation requires all 492 native cases, including four new ward/writing/save regressions, every focused job, all architecture and writing proofs, two additional native GPU views, and the actual socket multiplayer/reconnect expedition. Use `/oldesthouse test vignette` and its completion to place the institute's test door; ordinary saved visits receive the guarded repair after leaving the institute. Exact-source validation is pending.

The image-generation specification was a single 4-by-4 edge-to-edge hospital material atlas, with hard-edged 32-pixel logical tiles: sage intact paint, peeling paint, water-stained paint, ivory paint; glazed dado, ivory linoleum, gray linoleum, acoustic ceiling; chipped enamel, charcoal steel, olive vinyl, crazed ceramic; sage cabinet enamel, ivory laminate, ribbed frosted glass and a fluorescent diffuser. No perspective, labels, realism or baked vignette lighting. Export uses cropping, nearest-neighbour resizing and palette reduction. Meshes are authored to the actual native collision dimensions.
