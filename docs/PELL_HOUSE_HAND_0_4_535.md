The owner, 12 November: "start by finishing each unfinished vignette". The Pell house (`docs/PELL_HOUSE_PROPOSAL.md`) has its first
visit whole, and its second, a week in, has the afternoon, June's swim at first light (`docs/PELL_HOUSE_WEEK_0_4_533.md`) and the knife

and the second of Gail's marks, from "Being framed". The tapes, the freezer and the journal's false entries come after. The House still

## The laundry

- **A week in, in the late afternoon**, a while after June has called hello (from twenty past five by the house's clock, thirty
  seconds after her hello at the soonest), with June at her stove and not talking: "I'll just put a wash on, love. Won't be a minute." She goes
  through the kitchen's arch, along the hall and into the laundry, opening its door as she comes to it, and stands a step inside,
  facing the washing machine.
- **The hour holds** from when she goes until she is back at her stove. Dinner waits for her.
- **Anyone in the laundry** she asks out: "Out you come a minute, love. Let me get the wash on." (again every five seconds while they
  stay). She does not shut a door on anybody, and waits while anybody stands in either of its doorways.
- **Once it is hers** she shuts both its doors: the one from the hall and the back door to the yard.
- **If she cannot have it to herself within a minute** (somebody stays in it, keeps a doorway, keeps opening a door, or could see her the
  whole time), the wash keeps: "It'll keep." She goes back to her stove, and nothing happens this visit.

## Off screen

- **Shut in**, should a reader open either door or come in, she sees to it again as above. While anybody awake, spectators too, could
  see her (as for the knife: `PellNight.seen`), it waits.
- **Three seconds after she is shut in, unseen,** a door slams, hard, on something, and she cries out (`pell.hand`, "A door slams, and a
  cry"). Every reader within 24 blocks is told: inside the house "A door slams at the back of the house, hard. June cries out.", outside
  it "Over at the house a door slams, hard, and somebody cries out."
- **Nobody sees it.** What happened in the laundry is never shown.
- **Whoever was nearest the laundry's door from the hall** when she cried out, anywhere at the house, is the one she will say did it.

## Her hand

- **Two and a half seconds after the cry** she opens the laundry door and comes out. To whoever is within earshot: "It's nothing. I
  caught it in the door." Anyone who can see her then is told: "June's right hand is wrapped in a tea towel. She holds it against her."
- **She goes back to her stove** and keeps it wrapped for the rest of the visit: a tea towel round her right hand from the knuckles to
  the wrist, white with a blue stripe, its end tucked in at the back, a mark coming through it on the front.
- **The first hands on her** after it get a word about it, once each: "Don't fuss, love. It's only a knock." / "I've had worse." / "Mind
  yourself round that laundry door, love. It swings shut." Then her week's lines as before.
- **When the visit begins again**, her hand is as it was and nobody is to blame.

- **A reader can now have two marks**, one for the knife and one for the hand. What three do comes later.

## The week's call, after the hand

When June has somebody to say did it, the week's call is about it too.

- **June first.** After the knife: "And yesterday one of them slammed the laundry door on my hand." / "No, it's only bruised. I'll live."
  Without the knife: "Well. Not trouble, Gail. Not really." / "One of them slammed the laundry door on my hand yesterday." / "No, it's
  only bruised. I'll live." Then: "Tilly's eating well. She's a different girl." / "Hang on, I'll put them on."
- **Gail asks each reader about each thing in turn**, the knife first, one at a time, before her goodbye. After the knife she begins
  "And June's hand"; on its own, "June's told me about her hand".
- **To the reader June says did it**, Gail: "...She says you slammed the laundry door on it." / "Did you?" On offer: "I never touched the
  door.", "I wasn't even in the house." (only if they were not, when she cried out), "She did it herself.", or saying nothing.
- **To anybody else**, Gail: "...She says one of you slammed the laundry door on it." / "Did you see what happened, love?" On offer: "I
  heard it. I didn't see it." (only if they heard it), "It wasn't me.", "I don't want to stay here." (unless they have just said so about
  the knife), or saying nothing.
- **Gail:**

  If anybody said she did it herself: "They'll say anything when they're like this." Then: "I'll keep an eye. I always do." / "Next week,
  then. Bye now, love." Hung up, if anybody said they want to leave: "Give it time, love. That's all."
- **A reader who goes off with the receiver** has said nothing more, about either.
- **With neither the knife nor the hand**, the week's call is as it was.

## How it is made

- **`PellHand`** has the afternoon: `tick` (from the settled afternoon in `PellVisit.evening`, once June has called hello; true while
  she is away), its stages (`NOT_YET`, `TO_LAUNDRY`, `SHUTTING`, `INSIDE`, `CRIED`, `BACK`, `DONE`, and `KEPT` when the wash keeps), its
  timings (`AT` 11400 on the house's clock, `BEAT` 60, `AFTER` 50, `ASK` 100, `KEEPS` 1200), `HEARD` 24, where she stands inside
  (`INSIDE_SPOT`), her ways there and back, and the laundry's two doors.
- **The household** keeps where it has got to (`hand`), when she got to the laundry (`handAt`) and last shut herself in (`shutAt`), whom
  she will say did it (`blamed`), how many of her lines about it a hand on her has had (`handSaid`), and what each reader told Gail about
  it (`toldHand`). `PellVisit.clock` holds the hour while `PellHand.holds`; `PellVisit.restart` undoes it all.
- **Her hand** is a second synced number on the literary actor, `marks` (saved as "Marks0535"), with one bit, `PellPeople.WRAPPED`.
  `PellPeople.dressed(phase, gesture, marks)` picks June's skin `literary_pell_june_hand` while it is set: her day clothes, with the tea
  towel wrapped round the lower part of her right arm and her hand (`tools/paint_character_skins.py`, catalogue entry and phase 195 in
  `character_art.json`). Protocol 187: the actor's synced data has one more field.
- **The slam** is one new sound, `pell.hand` (`tools/generate_pell_audio.py`): a dull thump, a crack and a rattle, then the cry (a
  rising voice with its own breath), an intake and a breath out. Subtitled "A door slams, and a cry".
- **The call** (`PellCall`) now asks about what June has told Gail one thing at a time (`topics`: `ABOUT_KNIFE`, then `ABOUT_HAND`), with
  each answer kept apart; `PellTexts.gailHandAsks` and `PellTexts.gailHand` have Gail's lines.
- **For tests:** `PellHand.beforeHand` (the afternoon just before she goes), `PellHand.skip` (an afternoon on which she does not go), and
  `PellHand.blamed` (the visit as the laundry leaves it, this reader the one she says did it, away or not).
