# Borrasca: the two oaks where the street begins (0.4.472)

The owner, 11 November, with a picture looking up Drisking's street between two bare log posts:

> are these intentional? They're uneven, as like, gateway arches

They were not intended as they stood. They are the two trees the friend hides behind in her hide-and-seek on the first walk
(`BorrascaPlay`). Each had been built as three oak logs and nothing else: no crown. One stood on the street's east edge and
the other nearly in the middle of the road, so they were not the same distance from anything, and from the square they read as
the two posts of a gate with no top.

## What they are now

- Two oaks, one on each verge, the same distance either side of the street's middle, where the street leaves the square.
- Each has a trunk seven blocks tall and a round crown. The crowns begin four blocks over the road, so nothing of either tree
  is in the roadway or low over it.
- The friend's game is as it was: tagged, she tells the reader to turn and count, and hides behind the oak on the far side of
  the street from where the reader stands. Her hiding places are on the verges behind the trunks.
- The first of the street's lamp poles stood where the east oak now is. It stands three blocks nearer the square.

Kendra's bike stands under the west oak, as it did beside the old post.

### What the local run found

- **The first oaks were too low.** At five blocks tall their crowns hung over the road's edge, and a rider hopping Kendra's
  bike under the west one came down wrong: the bike's case failed. The trunks are seven blocks now, and the case that checks the
  street holds the road clear to four blocks up.

## What I have not seen

The street itself. I changed it from the owner's picture and the builder's code, and the cases check where every block is. I
have not looked at it from the square as the owner did.
