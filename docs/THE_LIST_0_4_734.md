# The list, part 9c: the House of Leaves (0.4.734)

The last of what *The list* leaves (`docs/THE_LIST_PLAN.md`, part 9). No protocol, layout or Witness change.

A reader whose account of the four nights has been read can find it in their own House of Leaves: a fact of its own kind, `listed`,
told in one of three ways, by how their nights left the list:

- "A town kept a list five names long. You left it {x}."
- "In a town on the high plains there was a list of five names. You left it {x}."
- "Four nights running it was ten o'clock in Lamesa. You left the list {x}."

where {x} is, from the record only: "with names crossed off", "with every name struck", "with no name crossed off", "with every name
gone", "with some names gone", "on the first bus out", "where Stace stopped you" or "in somebody else's hands". In the margin: "You were
in a town with a list in it. In the margin, five short lines, some of them struck through."

A reader who has not finished the four nights has nothing of it in their book.

Checked in `theTownAfter`.

With this, every part of the plan is built. What is still a stand-in is listed in `docs/THE_LIST_PLAN.md`.
