# New builds only: the world migrations removed (0.4.199)

The owner, October 31, 2026: "The mod is not yet being publicly shared, it doesn't need backwards compatibility", then "enact the removal if you're confident it will help and you won't break the mod in the process."

## What changed

**The builder.** `LabyrinthBuilder` no longer upgrades a saved layout in place. The thirty version thresholds that said which slots each layout had added, the domestic and architectural re-dressing of standing places, the rebuild-in-place of the elk lot, the trailer, the institute and Proofrock, the lake migration out of the interior and the outdoor relocation are gone. A layout saved by an earlier version now gains only the places it lacks: a place stands if the carve record (`labyrinth_places`, kept across layout bumps) lists it or, for layouts saved before that record existed, if it registered its entry door. Nothing standing is carved or dressed again. A fresh world carves exactly as before, in the order explorers can reach it.

**Removed.** The classes that existed only to change older worlds: `ElkUpgrade`, `WallNotesRepairs`, `VignetteHandlingUpgrade`, `PlaytestSceneRepairs`, `GhostsSetFinish`, `GhostsSetDressing`, `ProofrockRepairs`, `CaverRedesign`, `NurserySupport0492`, `CourtyardChess0492`, `ReviewScenery0491`, `GoatmanTrailerUpgrade`, `StreetlightUpgrade`, `PlaytestFixes`, `NightWardMaterials` (the 0.4.199 night ward builds its own materials, so the refit it also ran on a fresh ward replaced nothing). Their checkpoints and the calls that marked fresh builds as already done went with them.

**Audited before removal.** Each deleted or trimmed class was checked for a caller on the fresh-build path, not only the saved-world tick: the authored approach dressing, the plain's ground, the Goatman throat, the connector cap, the courtyard's chess tables and stone exterior, the cottage's chess corner, Camp Blood's decor, the lot's cars and streetlights, the institute's signs, shelf and attic roof and the Zampanò cabinets are all placed by their builders, so the walkers that also set them in saved worlds were no-ops on a fresh one. One pass was not: `PlaytestScenery130`'s furnishing of the cabin, the quiet room and the Last Stop ran only through the polish-time call every fresh build shares, and the cabin case caught its loss. It stays, as a fresh-build pass. The lakes' once-only wood settling keeps its tick-time retry too, since a build's own pass may wait on entity sections or a camera.

**Cut down to their fresh-build halves.** `PlaytestScenery130` (the plan and its guarded application inside the room's own build; the saved-room walker and its cursor go), `LakeLandscape` (the lake and its town stay; the migration and its login catch go), `OutdoorRelocation` (only the outdoor bounds remain), `DomesticHallUpgrade` (the halls' fabric pass stays, called as a hall is built), `HouseFurnishings` (the manor and halls are furnished as they are built; the saved re-decoration and note reduction go), `LabyrinthDomestic`, `BarnFarm`, `MotherOfStrays` (the den's gallery stairs are built with the den), `LabyrinthHazards` (the flooded hall's cold repair), `ScenePolish` and `SceneCraft` (polish and composition happen once, at build; the walkers over existing worlds and the late targeted repairs go), `WhaleHospital`, `ChildRoom`, `CrimsonGallery`, `CourtyardFountain`, `HarriganFinish`, `FoyerRefit`, `ArchiveReaderFiles`, `DinerDetails`, `ElkFanRoom`. Seven of these had no event handler left and are no longer bus subscribers.

**Kept.** Everything a fresh build uses, and the data-format tolerance that costs nothing: older SavedData shapes, older entity NBT, older Witness records and hotel custody. Also kept, for a later pass: the uncanny rooms' and vignette interiors' refit walkers (their tests also produce the environment proofs' views), the domestic lives' appends, `SceneReview`, `PlaytestSceneReview` and `SceneHuntReview`'s shared machinery, `SceneSupportRepairs`, `PlaytestScenes0488`, `StaceyCover`, and the great staircase's own carve versioning in `FinaleArchitecture`.

**Tests.** The cases that simulated an older layout or drove a saved-world repair are gone: the layout appends (versions 14 to 33, some bounded at 12,000 ticks), the saved refits and rebuilds, the lake migration, the relocation. Cases that mixed a fresh check with a saved repair keep the fresh half: the trailer's fixtures, door frame and window; the fresh town's streetlights and parked car; the diner's controls; the archive's seven named files; the courtyard's fish; the foyer's unhinged hall. `NightWardTests`, `NoticePostTests`, `FloodedUpgradeTests` and `LakeSettlementTests` are removed whole, and the `the_oldest_house_nightward` namespace with them. The architecture case that asserted an in-place upgrade now asserts that an older layout's carve finds every place standing and adds nothing.

## Why it helps

- The old-save appends were the slowest and most time-sensitive fixtures in the suite; three of the last twenty runs failed on one.
- About 3,500 lines of code and tests whose only job was to change worlds nobody has.
- Every remaining pass runs at build time, where a test can see it.

## What a saved world sees

A world saved by this or a later version and opened after a layout bump: its standing rooms as they were, the new places carved as the reader approaches, and no re-dressing. A world saved by an earlier version than this one: the same, except the six quiet rooms (which have no entry door and predate the carve record) are carved once more. Nothing older is supported.
