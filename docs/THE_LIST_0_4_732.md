# The list, part 8c: the rules tried for real (0.4.732)

The last of Amber's side (`docs/THE_LIST_PLAN.md`, part 8). Amber's notebook opens with three rules from the tapes. The one who went in
tested the rest on night two; a reader on Amber's side can now try the tapes' three during a hunt. Each is tried once by each reader and
kept in their own record. No protocol, layout or Witness change.

## The three rules

- **"Light keeps him back."** Wrong. A torch or lantern held in either hand, the one who went in up and within eight blocks, in the
  reader's look (twenty-five degrees) with nothing between them, for three seconds without a break: "You hold the light full on it. It
  doesn't look away, and it doesn't stop. That one's wrong."
- **"He can't come in unless you ask him in."** Wrong. A door the one who went in breaks down, house or steel, with the reader within
  eight blocks of it: "Nobody asked it in. It came in anyway. That one's wrong."
- **"Only at night."** Right. A reader in town when first light ends a hunt (not when the one who went in lies down on their bed):
  "First light, and it's gone. Only at night. That one was right."

## The account

For a reader on Amber's side, as was so: "Amber's tapes said light kept it back. I held a light right on it, and it came on anyway.";
"The tapes said it couldn't come in unless you asked. Nobody asked. I watched it come in."; "They said only at night. That one held. At
first light it was gone."

## Single and multiplayer

- **Alone**, none of this happens: there is nobody on Amber's side.
- **With others**, each reader tries each rule for themselves and keeps only their own. The one who went in and **spectators** try
  nothing.
