# The house of Usher, built whole — 0.4.181

The owner asked for the next pass to expand a vignette that still needed it, and left the choice to Claude. Usher had been a dark-panelled box with a coffin in it, dressed by the general scenery passes. It is now built whole, in its own pixels, as the Masque, Bly and Winchester were. This is stage A: the house itself. Its story is unchanged, and stage B (Roderick, the storm and the fall) follows.

## What is there

`labyrinth/UsherHouse.java` builds everything inside the room's bounds (x -18..18, y -4..13, z -42..0).

- **The approach.**
  - From the reader's door a podzol bank runs down to a black tarn, five blocks of water over ooze.
  - A stone causeway crosses the tarn to the archway.
  - Dead trunks stand in the water and on the bank, and grass and ferns grow along it.
  - There is no lamp and no moon. A faint light hangs in the vapour over the water, and the night closes round the house.
- **The front.**
  - Grey stone furred with fungus, with a row of battlements against the night.
  - A pointed archway three blocks wide.
  - Two storeys of long, vacant windows.
  - The fissure, which runs from the roof down the wall in a zigzag until it is lost in the tarn. Each cell of the crack shows where it enters and leaves that block.
- **The hall.**
  - Tapestried walls, a black floor and a fretted ceiling nine blocks up.
  - Armorial trophies, candelabra, and the family portrait on the west wall.
  - The vault register on an iron-bound desk.
  - Arched ways open into the galleries on either side.
  - A railed stair in the east corner goes down past the studio to the vault's landing.
  - A pair of heavy black doors leads into the studio.
- **The vault.**
  - It is low and damp, under the studio, behind a door of copper-sheathed iron at the foot of the stair.
  - Madeline's coffin lies there, with candles.
  - On each return to a closed lid, the crack follows the scratching across the vault's west wall.
- **The studio.**
  - It is lofty and dark, with draperies on the walls.
  - **Windows.** High, pointed windows stand above the galleries' roofs, open on the night and out of reach from the black floor.
  - **Roderick's things.** His chair on a worn rug, his easel and its painting, his guitar, and books strewn on the floor.
  - **The rest.** Two candelabra, the one hearth in the east wall with its chimney, his spinet, and a writing desk with ink.
- **The galleries.**
  - Two lower wings with fretted ceilings, stone roofs and the night above them.
  - A passage across the back, with trophies, a light apiece and the press.
  - The way on, at the back.
- **The account.** It waits on a cut stump on the bank by the water, where the reader came in.

## Its own pixels

- **The block.** One `usher` block, `UsherBlock`, has 18 kinds × facing × stage, 288 states in all:
  - **Cubes:** stone, fissure (two kinds holding the seven crack shapes, the last one opened wide), floor, tapestry, fret, vault, causeway, mud, night.
  - **Shaped pieces:** window (lower and upper), drapery, easel, guitar, books, candelabra (light 11), armorial, chair.
- **The art.** `tools/draw_usher_art.py` draws the 28 textures, the models and the blockstate at Minecraft's own sixteen-pixel scale. CI runs its `--check`.
- **The register's stand.** The vault register stands on a seventh reading stand, `vault_register`: a black oak slope desk strapped in iron, its pages in columns of names and dates, with a red seal at the foot. It is drawn by `tools/draw_reading_stands.py` and added to the native reading-stand views. None of the six earlier stands' pixels change.

## What stays

- The story is unchanged:
  - the lid is chosen at the coffin, which is still at `LiteraryRooms.COFFIN`;
  - the scratching and the crack on return to a closed lid;
  - the woman found standing in the wing (the east gallery) after an open one;
  - the account.
- The co-op vault account is unchanged.
- No Witness change: Usher is still one understanding source. Witness stays 55 / 42 / two kinds / three endings.
- **The scenery passes leave it alone.** As with the other rooms built whole, SceneCraft, ScenePolish (and SceneDressing with it), SceneReview, ScenePlaytestRepairs and the VignetteInteriors refit all leave it as built. Its spinet, press, writing desk and portrait are placed by the builder itself.
- **New builds only.** Saved worlds keep the Usher they have.
- **Layout and protocol unchanged:** layout 51, protocol 101.
