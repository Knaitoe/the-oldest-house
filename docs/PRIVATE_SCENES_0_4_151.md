Since 0.4.153 a reader who finds the confession, the Holy Rabbit, the Wheel house, the diner or the split-feed bedroom taken gets a copy of their own instead of being turned away (`docs/SCENE_COPIES_0_4_153.md`). The rule below still holds for the other three, and for every scene once the server's copies run out.

They also agreed to four more: the Whale institute, the phone in the canoe, the diner (the second waitress) and the split-feed bedroom (missing sleep). ## Why these are private

- **Harrigan:** reading to him, his phone, the texts and calls on your own screen, and burying or keeping the phone are a one-on-one bond.
- **The confession:** your answers, in your own hand, and a last chapter that counts your own kills.
- **The Holy Rabbit:** one child's days with Father, and the rabbit you named.
- **The Wheel house:** every room measures you against Mother.
- **The Whale institute:** letters answered in your own handwriting, quoting your letters.
- **The phone in the canoe:** your recording and your memory.
- **The diner:** the waitress addresses and serves one diner, and answering her fails that reader.
- **The split-feed bedroom:** the CCTV shows your own body.

## What private means here

The backseat, the silo, Borrasca, the family and old-cabin copies and the Red Room were already private, and each has a copy for every reader. These eight scenes stand once, so they are private in a second way: one reader at a time.

- **Friends do not follow each other in.** A group at the door is dealt a scene they can share instead, as at the backseat (`GroupCrossings`).
- **One reader at a time.** While a living reader is in one of these scenes, the door into it opens onto an ordinary hall for anyone else, with "Someone is already in <the story's title>. It will wait for you." That reader's door becomes a way on (`LabyrinthDealer.openWayOn`), and the scene stays theirs to find later; they have not been in it and have not begun its story.
  - A spectator in the scene does not hold it.
  - An operator's test door still goes in.
  - Where the door has no hall to open onto, it stays shut.
- **Nothing else changes.** Each story, its resolution and its Witness line are as before.
