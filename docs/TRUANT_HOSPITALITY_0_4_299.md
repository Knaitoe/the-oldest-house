# Johnny calls in a favour

At a later claimant meeting by his lamp, Johnny can use the bread or coffee the reader took before. He can still offer either in a genuinely helpful meeting.

After a companion actually takes the lamp hall's far door on the same route, Johnny may ask the bread recipient where they went. He makes the accusation aloud and starts toward that door. The reader can warn the companion or step in. Another reader can intervene in person. Johnny stops at the door if he gets that far.

Johnny can offer the coffee recipient a second cup. If they set it down to go, he reaches first and catches their wrist only if they stay within reach. A companion can interrupt, or the brief restraint wears off. Anyone under twenty passes in the room prevents either escalation.
