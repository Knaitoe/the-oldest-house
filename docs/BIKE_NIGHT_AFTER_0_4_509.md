# Bike night 26: what follows (0.4.509)

The proposal (`docs/BIKE_NIGHT_PROPOSAL.md`, "Success: out the back gate"): "**What follows:** Gus is there if he
made it (in co-op, the other riders who made it); the taken cap is hanging on the gate; the bullies' three bikes lie
abandoned by the front gate, and the bullies are nowhere."

And, as answered when the graveyard was planned: the three are "readable as having dropped their bikes and run."

Until now the three were taken with their bikes, and nothing was left of them or of the cap.

## The three's bikes

What each of the three rode lies on its side on the pavement outside the cemetery's front gate
(`BikeNightGraveyard.after`). Tully's and Ray-Ray's are west of the gateway. Duff's is east of it, where he stopped,
under the street lamp that stands there. No two point the same way. They are clear of the gateway and off the lane.

Each is set down the first moment after its rider is taken that nobody could be looking at the place: the same rule
he was taken by. A reader who stands on the lane looking at the gate sees nothing arrive. They turn round, and turn
back, and the bikes are there.

The last of the three to go is the one at the fence. The wheels of his bike are still going round, with the tick of
its freewheel, and they run down over about seven seconds once somebody is near. The other two are still.

A bike lying there is nobody's: no hand takes it and nobody rides it. The three are nowhere.

Whoever comes within eight blocks of them has it in their record, and their account says so: "Their three bikes were
lying on the pavement outside the cemetery's front gate. Nobody was with them." (Or two, or one, if that was all that
lay there: one of the three who got a reader's bike in the town rode off with it and never came to the gate.)

## The cap

If Tully had Gus's cap on when he was taken, it is on a nail in the back gate's east post, on the side a rider comes
up to from the Hollow, at a child's eye. It is put there the same way: the first moment nobody could be looking at the
post. Nothing says who hung it.

It is the cap the painter gives Gus, as a fitting of its own (`CalderBlock.Kind.HAT`, drawn by
`tools/draw_calder_art.py`): its crown, the pale front panel with its letter, the dark bill. A hand on it:
"A ball cap, on a nail in the gatepost. There is a name inside the band, in pen: Gus." (In the park he had shouted
that his mother wrote his name in it.)

In a night in which Tully never got the cap, there is none to hang.

## Gus

Gus comes off the Hollow's line after his friend, as before. If his cap is on the post, he rides to it first, stops
beside it, and, when his friend is within sixteen blocks, takes it down and has it on. Then he says two things, over
his head as he says everything:

- "That's my cap. That's my mom's writing in it. So it's mine."
- "Tully had it on. I'm not asking."

Then he rides after his friend again. It goes in the record of whoever was within twenty-four blocks, and their
account has it: "Gus's cap was on a nail in the gatepost. He took it down and looked inside it, and put it on."

"(in co-op, the other riders who made it)": they are simply there. Nothing was needed for that.

## The night over

The bikes go with the rest of the three's when the night resets, and the cap comes down. A cap left on the post by a
night that ended with the post out of reach comes down in the next one, unseen, as it went up.

## How it is drawn

A pack bike has a laid state (`PackBike.lay`): the renderer turns it eighty degrees over and lifts it a little, so
that its lower pedal and the end of its bars are what rest on the ground. The proof has a page for it
(`native-bike-night-after.png`): the three bikes as they lie, and the cap on a post.

## The cases

One new case, 97 in the Bike night namespace, and two older ones go further.

- **New:** two readers, one looking at the front gate and one at the back gate's post. The three are taken. For three
  seconds nothing is put anywhere. They turn their backs: three bikes lie where they should, each one of the three's,
  on its side, pointing as it fell, with nobody on it and nobody's to take; the last one's wheels going round and the
  others' not; the cap on the post, facing the Hollow, in nobody's way; a hand on it is told whose it is. The wheels
  run down with somebody by. The record and the account have the bikes, for the reader who came on them and not the
  other. The night over, nothing lies there and nothing hangs there. In another night, with no cap on Tully, three
  bikes and no cap, and a cap left from before comes down.
- **Gus's ride through the Hollow** now ends with the cap on the post before he is off the line, him riding to it,
  taking it down, saying both lines in order (never in chat), and stopping by his friend; then out by the gate as
  before.
- **The three at the graveyard** now ends, after they are gone unseen, with what they rode lying outside the gate.

## What the cases found

- Duff's bike was to lie where a street lamp stands. It lies beside the lamp now, and the case requires a block of
  clear room every way from each bike's middle.
- Gus never left the Hollow's line while his friend stood at its end: he keeps his distance from them along it. He
  comes off it when they go on toward the gate, which is where a reader goes. The case sends his friend on.

## Not built, not seen, and left with the owner

- **Which gate the cap is on** is a reading. "The taken cap is hanging on the gate" is in the list of what follows
  riding out of the far gate, so it is on the back gate's post. If the owner meant the front gate, with the bikes, it
  is one constant.
- **Gus taking it down himself** is an addition. The proposal only hangs it there. If the owner would rather the
  reader takes it down and gives it to him, or that it stays, say so.
- **The wheels still going round** are an addition.
- **Numbers:** where the bikes lie; eight blocks to have come on them; sixteen for Gus to take his cap; seven seconds
  for the wheels.
- None of this has been seen in a client except the proof's page.
- Still not built: the keepsake (the owner's call), dawn for a reader the Hollow has had, the headstone and the
  rusting bike, and the bike chained to a school fence.
