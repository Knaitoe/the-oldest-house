# Borrasca — development candidate 0.4.97

The House opens onto an original, restrained adaptation of C. K. Walker's four-part **Borrasca**. Drisking's crime remains human. The impossible entry belongs to the House. There is no supernatural boss, assault animation, assault audio, nudity, victim item or reproductive simulation. This adaptation uses adult cast/victim records and newly written dialogue and papers; it does not reproduce the story's prose or attempt Part V.

The source and place names are credited to [C. K. Walker's original](https://ck-walker.com/2016/04/12/borrasca/). The realization, dialogue, documents, geometry, atlas and synthetic sound design are new work for this mod.

## Art and sound

`textures/entity/borrasca_atlas.png` is a new opaque 4×4 painted atlas: four faces; four clothing materials; denim, trousers, skin and hair; timber, galvanized metal, rust and paper. Explicit native face UVs fit its islands. Existing PNG files are not changed. The same atlas supplies the new block fittings and the native rotating machine. Joints use the existing native humanoid movement sampler.

Image-generation prompt: a square, edge-to-edge sixteen-tile atlas with no gutters or labels; four neutral adult faces, forest plaid/ochre/tan/indigo fabrics, denim/charcoal/skin/hair, and timber/steel/rust/cream paper; restrained painterly pixel-art diffuse materials. The original generated image is retained unchanged in the repository. Native block atlas registration is explicit.

`tools/compose_borrasca.py` deterministically composes original mono grinder, motor wind-down, timber creak and wind recordings without sampled speech or music. The muffled grinder is a phase-aligned low-pass copy of the same take. The fixed refinery position drives spatial panning, distance gain and enclosure filtering on the client. Power can be switched off at the actual machine. Vanilla music, including already-playing music, is suppressed while the reader occupies the leased site; departure/death/dimension changes clear the lease. There is no score. All sounds have subtitles and follow the Ambient/Master controls.

## Goatman visibility and harsher knocks (October 15 follow-up)

The owner still could not see the Goatman circling the trailer, and asked for harsher knocking. Diagnosis from the source:

- Each side pane is one block wide in a one-block wall, so a body just outside it can only be seen from a wedge of about ±18° inside the trailer. The original body waited the same 26–56 ticks at all five side panes whether or not any reader could see them, and a watched glimpse ended after 18 watched ticks.
- The private "tearing round" laps (0.4.84) ran on the ground at 0.8 blocks a tick. The figure's skull spans 1.69–2.13 above its feet and the sill is at y 2, so it passed below the glass in about one tick per pane: heard at every window, seen at none.
- The vigil knock master was 92% energy below 150 Hz with nothing above 2 kHz; the hammer was similar. Both were soft, padded thumps made louder.

Changes:

- **Original body.** At each side pane `GoatmanWindowCircuit.start` checks whether any living reader's actual camera has a clear native glazing ray to the face. A pane someone can see keeps the 26–56 tick wait for a look; a pane nobody can see gets the slap and an 8–20 tick pause, so the night's time goes where it can be seen and unattended circuits finish sooner. Once a reader is actually looking, the body stays at the glass for at least 40 watched ticks (up to 80 after the first look), laughing without a sound (the existing heave tell) while watched.
- **Private lap.** At a pane its reader can see from where they stand, the lap figure vaults up onto the real tire beneath it, so its head and shoulders fill the glass, slams the pane, waits up to 46 ticks for a look, and once watched stares back, laughing, for 24 watched ticks (hitting the glass again on the eighth), then drops back to its lane past the window. Panes the reader cannot see are still slapped at a run. During the vigil the figure gets the same light floor (block light 7) as the original body's window silhouette. Lap lifetime is 1,200 ticks.
- **Sound.** `tools/generate_goatman_harsh_impacts.py` synthesizes new `knock_vigil`, `claw_vigil` and `hammer_vigil` masters as blows on a thin tin camper: knuckle/fist crack (1.2–9 kHz), the skin's short sagging boom, an inharmonic detuned sheet-metal ring, the pane or door chattering in its frame, and driven saturation. The knock is four hard raps, the last the heaviest; the claw is a hit then a stick-slip nail screech across the glass, twice; the hammer is one full-body blow with a long clang and a rattle of things inside. All stay mono 44.1 kHz Vorbis, decode without clipping (peak about 0.94), and are about 9 dB (knock) and 13 dB (hammer) louder A-weighted than the 0.4.94 masters, with roughly 40% of the knock's A-weighted energy above 1 kHz (previously 9%). `generate_goatman_score.py` now calls this generator instead of re-boosting the old recordings. Event names, subtitles, gain ≥3 and 48-block attenuation are unchanged.
