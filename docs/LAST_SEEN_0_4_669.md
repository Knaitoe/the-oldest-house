# Last Seen 3: more of them (0.4.669)

This is the third of the five parts of *Last Seen* (`docs/LAST_SEEN_PLAN.md`). It is built to the recommended decisions in `docs/THREE_VIGNETTES_PROPOSAL_2026_11_17.md`, which the owner approved on 17 November.

Part 2 (`docs/LAST_SEEN_0_4_668.md`) gave the reader the archive and the first of the ten. Part 3 is the second day. Eight of them are in the park, families come, and the city comes after them. It is still not a Witness source; that is part 5.

## The second day

A reader who has matched the first of them (their own `Days` at least one) comes back to the second day. With two or more readers, the day is the furthest any of them has reached. A reader who has not matched her is on the second day too while they are with someone who has, and nothing on it counts toward their own first day.

The second day starts with:

- the table bare, and every folder in its bay;
- the front door open;
- the park's hour set to the morning, then moving through the afternoon to dusk over the first four minutes of the day's own clock.

The hour is the place's own (`LastSeenTimePayload`, clock mode 12), sent to each reader on arrival and every two seconds after. The rest of the House keeps its own time.

### The eight

Eight of the ten are in the park, each at their own spot, at child scale, facing their own way. Each is made only where no reader can see the spot; if a reader is looking, that one is not there yet.

| Child | Spot | Facing |
|---|---|---|
| Rocío | the fountain's rim | the office |
| Tomás | the railing across from the bus stop | the bus stop |
| Micaela | by the north gate | the gate |
| Brian | outside the kiosk, its shutter down behind him | away from it |
| Daiana | the bench under the plane trees | the path |
| Lautaro | the playground's old gap | the swings |
| Abril | alone on the bandstand | east |
| Joel | the corner of the railing | the far side of the avenue |

Yanina and Santino never come. Their folders stay on their shelves.

Each of the eight is kept to their spot and turned their own way, unless they are holding their folder or being led.

### Matching them

Matching works as it did for the first of them:

- A reader takes a child's own folder from its bay and brings it to them.
- The child takes it into their own hand and looks down at it for two seconds.
- Then they look up at the one who brought it and say the one thing that is theirs.
- Three seconds later they give it back, or put it back on its shelf if that reader has gone.

Each match on the second day is counted for that reader (`Second`). When a reader has matched four, the second day is theirs (`Days` at least two).

A reader who comes near one of them is told how they stand, once a day.

Daiana can be matched before her father comes and after he brings her back, not while he is leading her.

### Something to eat

Each of the ten will take something to eat that is held out, hold it in the other hand, and not eat it. A second thing offered while they are holding the first is not taken.

### Cold

Touched with an empty hand, each is cold, and frost breathes off them. Once his grandmother has put his coat round him, Tomás is cold under the coat.

## The families

Each visitor is made only where no reader sees them, at the park's south-west gate, which they open themselves if it is shut. Spoken to while walking, they stop to answer, then go on. One of them leaves again only once no reader can see them go.

- **Joel's mother** (from 10 seconds into the day) stands on the far pavement of the avenue, opposite him, facing the park. She never crosses.
- **Tomás's grandmother** (from 20 seconds) comes in by the gate, his coat over her arms, and walks to him. Two seconds after she reaches him, she puts it round his shoulders (`COAT_MARK`; he is drawn in it from then on). Then she stays beside him, turned to him. Everyone within twenty blocks of Tomás sees it happen.
- **Daiana's father** (from 50 seconds) walks down the path to her bench and says "Come on." Two seconds later he takes her by the wrist and leads her out of the park, his hand back to hers and her arm out ahead to it. He waits while she is holding her folder. The two leave together, but only once neither is seen. An hour later in the day's clock (sixty seconds), he brings her back to the gate and leaves her there. From then on she stands at the gate, facing the avenue.

## The city

From 100 seconds:

- **The fence.** A wire fence goes up round the playground, a panel at a time, only where nobody sees it go up and never round a reader. It comes up two blocks high: welded mesh on a galvanised frame, two blocks to a panel, each joint on a precast foot, with a post at each corner and its half-panels turned to run along both sides. The old gap in the playground's own low fence closes last. Then Lautaro puts his hands to the mesh. Where the ring crosses grass or a flower, the fence goes up over it; it is put back when the day is put back. A cell where something solid already stands is left out, and the fence meets it. A cell someone is standing in waits until they move; one standing at its edge, as Lautaro stands at the old gap, does not hold it up.
- **The notices.** A notice is taped round each of the ten lamp posts, one at a time, only where nobody sees, facing the path the lamp stands by.
- **Mr. Ocampo** (from 130 seconds) comes in by the gate, walks round the fountain and up the path, through the front door, to the end of the counter facing Nora's desk.
  1. He tells her: "A child who comes back is a closed file."
  2. Three seconds later: "Close them, Nora. All of them."
  3. Two and a half seconds after that, Nora answers: "They stay open."
  4. Two seconds later he leaves the way he came.

## Dusk

At four minutes into the day, the light is going. Every one of them turns to face the avenue, and every reader in the place is told so.

## When nobody is there

When nobody has been in the place for ten seconds, it is put back as it was:

- the ten and the visitors are gone;
- the city's fence comes down and the notices come off the lamps;
- the three are in their chairs, every folder is in its bay, and the front door is shut.

What each reader matched, fed, saw and heard is saved to them.

## Single and multiplayer

- **Shared:**
  - the day, its clock and its hour;
  - the eight and where they stand;
  - the visitors, the fence and the notices;
  - who is holding their folder.
- **Each reader's own:**
  - which of them they matched on the second day (`Second`), and whether the day is theirs (`Days`);
  - whom they fed (`Fed`) and saw (`Saw`);
  - what each of the office's three, the visitors and Nora's second-day lines said to them (`Heard2`, `Family<n>`, `Phone`, `Counted`, `CoatHeard`);
  - whether they saw another reader match one of them (`SawMatched`).
- **Alone or together.** One reader can do the whole day alone. With two or more:
  - each holds their own folder;
  - one of the ten holds one folder at a time, so each reader brings it in turn;
  - each is counted for themselves;
  - something to eat given by one is held, and the next offered is refused.
- **Spectators** are never readers here. They are not counted as having seen anyone, and the families, the fence and the notices are made whatever a spectator is looking at.
