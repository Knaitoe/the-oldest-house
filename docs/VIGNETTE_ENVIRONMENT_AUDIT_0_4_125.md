## Standard and evidence

Last Stop establishes an actual place before its event: roadside materials, a bar with service, conversations, seats, games and music, vehicles that can be damaged, a working door and lights, and a field beyond them. The relevant standard is coherence and meaningful physical activity, rather than filling every empty floor cell. A workshop should look used for making models. A child's room needs a clear floor for its game. A distant desert figure needs distance. A copied home must remain the player's actual home.

The starting review examined the previous exact-source native scene corpus: 105 views from the successful `8cf14ef` build, including floor-specific views of the hotel, Barrett home, lake house, Borrasca and elk site. The merged starting tree is the same tested tree. The new pass adds fresh native construction/use/custody cases and fourteen focused native room cutaways and four floor-specific views, alongside the complete existing gameplay, model, client and reconnect checks. The focused job is `interior-gameplay`; it checks generator parity, real block use and collision, preservation of originals across a saved-room refit, and native rendered screenshots. Passing that job alone is not a complete release gate.

Full-bright cutaways omit actors and deliberately cut away roofs and walls. They establish the shipped geometry and material composition; they do not prove ordinary lighting, actor behavior or every possible playthrough. Those require the separate native gameplay/client checks. A generic audit that labels every decorative custom block as an interaction is not evidence of actual usability.

## Changes

Fourteen older rooms receive explicit, authored changes. Forty-nine original sixteen-pixel tiles support 69 material/fitting kinds, including 29 usable kinds. Some materials are shared where ordinary wood, brass, enamel or linen should be shared; scene identity comes from its own patterns, material combinations, arrangement, equipment and existing story. These are additive assets. No existing PNG or NPC sheet is repainted.

The new controls operate ordinary things: cupboards and cases expose modeled contents; lamps and the slide sorter change real emitted light; basins visibly fill and empty within their own fixture; a horse rocks; a basket opens; model tools move; instrument keys and the bell depress and reset; a music box opens with a brief chime. The recorder is an opening tape compartment, not an invented source of recorded lore. Model/collision geometry shares one checked cuboid definition, including rotated and opened states. New moving parts cannot close through a body.

Existing room actions remain the source of story progression. No new checklist, compulsory document, incidental note, repeating narration, reward stock, NPC, Witness requirement or story resolution is added. All existing custom furniture, finite source/ending objects and identities remain.

## Saved worlds and release requirements

Fresh construction receives the refit after its original composition. Previously built rooms use explicit recognized original states and a saved once-only checkpoint. The migration only visits already loaded, entity-resident, unwatched rooms, with a 256-cell / three-millisecond per-tick bound. It never runs either room's original construction callbacks. Changed cells, block entities, occupied positions, dropped originals and current controls are protected. Existing book, model, container and actor identities are not regenerated.

The native migration case starts from the old miniature workshop, retains its source and three model block entities by identity, places an edited wall and a nonempty original chest, leaves a body in a proposed furnishing cell, performs the sliced refit, and reloads the checkpoint with an already-open cabinet. The native room case constructs all fourteen rooms through their original builders/composition, requires every intended fitting and new partition cell to actually exist, operates each usable fitting and checks emitted light, original sources, key approaches, model fronts, interview shelf, low crawl and native jukebox, plus the new interview, kitchen and study openings. The third case checks every kind, direction and active state against native collision. The generator also fails on stale JSON, texture, geometry or registry output.

Release verification requires the complete exact-source suite, existing 105 native scene views and all existing real-client/reconnect proofs, as well as the new focused interior job. Review its eighteen refreshed native images before accepting the candidate. The audit is not a declaration that all possible player-built copied homes or all ordinary playthroughs have been manually exhausted.
