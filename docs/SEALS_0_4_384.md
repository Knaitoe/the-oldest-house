# The seals: a harder road to the staircase (0.4.384)

The owner: "I've found it too easy to get to the staircase." Until now the great staircase came to anyone twenty-two doors deep who had walked into two stories. Nothing on the way had to be overcome: every trial was one door among several and could be walked past.

This release builds R1 from [HALLS_TRIALS_AND_TIME_DESIGN.md](HALLS_TRIALS_AND_TIME_DESIGN.md). The owner left the open questions to judgement; the choices made are noted.

## The rule

The staircase is offered only to a reader who:

- is **twenty-four doors deep** (`LabyrinthPacing.STAIRCASE_DEPTH`, was twenty-two);
- has **three marks**, one for each of three different kinds of trial (`TrialMarks.SEALS`);
- has heard **four stories to their end** (`TrialMarks.STORIES`), counted the way the account counts them (`WitnessAccount.count`). Rooms only visited no longer count. This answers the design's question 4: the old gate was two rooms visited.

`FinaleProgress.eligible` is now depth twelve and `TrialMarks.sealed`. The dealer still adds the depth (`LabyrinthDealer.finaleOffered`). A reader who has already found the staircase keeps it (`Discovered`), as before.

## Marks

A mark is earned by coming through a trial by its far door, once for each kind of trial.

- **What counts as a trial.** Every physical trial (`LabyrinthPacing.physicalTrial`): the flooded passage, the broken walkway, the room that ate light, the eight hazard rooms of 0.4.80, the passage that drew breath, the settling cellar, and the four yard hazards. Seventeen kinds in all.
- **Siblings.** A sibling counts as the room it varies (`HazardVariety.original`), so the bent cistern is a mark for the flooded passage, not a second one.
- **When.** The mark is given where the House already notices a trial completed: after a successful crossing of one of the trial's dealt doors, never its entry (`ExpeditionRhythm.crossedHazard`). Walking back out the way in earns nothing. A spectator earns nothing.
- **Each reader's own.** Marks are kept per reader (`trial_marks_0384`), in the order earned. Crossing together gives each reader their own mark.
- **Puzzles and anomalies.** Puzzle rooms (the Cold Pantry among them) and anomalies are not trials and give no mark.

## What the reader sees

All in grey italics, on the far side of the door:

- First mark: *Chalked on the frame as you pass through, a mark you did not make. One.*
- Second: *Another mark on the frame. Two.*
- Third, with four stories already heard: *A third mark on the frame. Somewhere below you, a stair is no longer kept from you.*
- Third, without: *A third mark on the frame. Somewhere below you, a stair waits until you have heard enough of the House's rooms to their end.*
- Any after that: *Another mark on the frame. The House keeps count.*

If the stories make up the seal after the marks, the next arrival says the same once: *Somewhere below you, a stair is no longer kept from you.* Either way it is said once (`Told`).

The design asked for a chalk sigil on the reader's map. There is no map item in the House, so the mark lives on the door frame, in that line, and in the account.

## The account

After "The account so far", the Witness account gains a page on the marks (`TrialMarks.pages`, at most 180 characters a page): *THE MARKS. I came through the flooded passage, the cold store and the storm field by the far door, each of them.* While the seal is incomplete it adds what is missing:

- fewer than three marks: *The House keeps its deepest stair from anyone with fewer than three marks.*
- marks enough, stories not: *The marks are enough. The stair still waits on the rooms I have not heard to their end.*

Nothing appears before the first mark.

## Also

- The advancement `twenty_doors` reads *Twenty-four doors deep: As deep as the stairs are kept.* Its id and place in the tree are unchanged.
- The dark halls are still the only halls from twenty-one; the staircase now waits three doors further into them.

## Not in this release

R2 (trials standing in the way of the deep halls) and the new trial rooms and halls follow. R3 (folds) and R4 (a slower first stretch) are left for a playtest.

## Tests

- New `FinaleTests.threeTrialMarksAndFourStoriesSealTheStaircase`:
  - every physical trial is a named kind and every kind is an original;
  - halls, stories and nothing earn no mark;
  - a sibling earns its original's mark, once;
  - marks keep their order;
  - the account's pages fit a book page and say what is missing;
  - three stories and a fourth only visited are not a seal, and the fourth heard to its end is;
  - sealed and deep, the stair is offered, and every mark without stories is not enough.
- `FinaleTests.optionalDiscoveryRequiresDepthAndPersonalExploration`: rooms visited no longer open the way; the seal does, for that reader alone.
- `RhythmTests.onlyACompletedHazardFarDoorArmsABreather` also checks the mark:
  - none for the retreat or the observer;
  - one for the far door;
  - no second for the same kind.
- `DarkHallTests` checks twenty-four (the case is renamed `...TheStaircaseWaitsDeeper`).
