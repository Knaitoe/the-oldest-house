catch at the occupied frame (in either order, slowly alone, faster with somebody at the other). Every original walks out; Dan and

## The two places

- The cellar's ratchet wheel and a catch low in the jamb of Daniel's frame (its east side). Only a reader who knows the third way
  (`KNOWS_THIRD`, the dead post in the cellar) can work either; anyone else finds a wheel and a frame.
- Each use lifts one step of eight. A step counts twice if somebody else worked the other place within the last three seconds. One
  hand can only go so fast: a reader's own uses are spaced. Working it alone is louder (the sill groans the length of the back wall).
- It cannot begin once E1 or E2 has begun, and starting it closes both.

## Severed

When both are clear, every reader in the house feels it go, Daniel's straps open and he is free, and the failed bodies come apart in
the plan's three ways: the one at the dead post begs for the frame back, the one at the hatch sits down where it is, and the one at
the jib door comes through it (lifting its latch from the house's side) along the back passage and the hall for Daniel, and puts its
hands flat on him. Vera says the house is let go.

Dan and Daniel walk by the real doors (Daniel steps straight out of his frame first) to the hall bench by the front door and sit.
The body on Daniel sits down after two blows from any reader, or by itself after twenty seconds there. When both men are sitting and
the body has sat, every reader within twelve blocks of them gets the closing image.
