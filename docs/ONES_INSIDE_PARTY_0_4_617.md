# The Ones Already Inside 17: party changes (0.4.617)

The plan's seventeenth slice (`docs/THE_ONES_ALREADY_INSIDE_PLAN.md`): "Party changes: late arrival, withdrawal, disconnects,
reconnects, the key's custody, everyone gone; solo." The House still does not deal the house.

New builds only. No layout, protocol or Witness change. The logic is `LodgingParty`. No new models, poses or textures.

## The key

A reader holding the key who walks out of the house with it, logs off, dies, or simply has not got it any more (dropped it, put it in
a box) puts it back where it lives: in Rae's coat if Rae gave it to them, in the cupboard under the stair if it was found there. A key
lying loose on any floor of the house goes back the same way. Checked once a second; a reader who logs back on in the house does not
have it, and asks Rae or opens the cupboard again like anyone else.

## Coming in late

A reader's first arrival is recorded. The first reader in is never late. A later reader who comes in once Rae has begun asking:

- before the crisis (the first attempt, or any of the four ends), comes in at the hall as usual, and Dan tells them in two lines what is
  going on; Rae asks them in their turn when they speak to her, as she asked the others;
- once the crisis is under way, comes in by the scullery's side door, inside among the pails, with nothing invented for them.

The account says which.

## Withdrawal, disconnects, everybody gone, solo

Already true of the earlier slices, checked here:

- A reader can leave by the front door at any time; everything they did is in their own record and their account is written from it.
- The house is reset only when it is built again. With nobody in it, its people do not tick: the chapter, where the four stand, the
  link, the taken and every half-done mechanism wait as they are. A reader who logs back in finds them so.
- Nothing one reader needs to do waits for a second: E1 can be agreed or walked by one, E2 held by one with the straps, E3 worked by
  one (slower and louder), E4 struck by one.
