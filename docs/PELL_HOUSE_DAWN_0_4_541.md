# The Pell house 19: dawn and the endings (0.4.541)

The owner, 12 November: "start by finishing each unfinished vignette". The Pell house (`docs/PELL_HOUSE_PROPOSAL.md`) has its first
two visits whole, and its last night runs to midnight in the pool, getting Tilly out, and the freezer (`docs/PELL_HOUSE_FREEZER_0_4_540.md`).
This is its nineteenth slice: first light, and the two endings that land there. From the proposal's "3. The last night":

> - **Dawn:** Gail's car at the curb, either way.

and from "Endings":

> Every ending is permanent. There is no second attempt, and the vignette is never dealt again to a reader whose ending has landed.
>
> - **What follows:** Gail's car is at the curb. Callum is sitting on the front step with his shoes on.
> - **The account** (survival kind) ends on the street, beyond the line. If the reader brought June to the freezer, it adds that she was
>   still sitting there when the car came.
> - **The keepsake** is Tilly's spare cane-tip, a small worn rubber thing, owner-bound.

> - **For a character Tilly** (solo, or co-op with a neighbour): Tilly stays; the older sibling is in Gail's car at dawn, and Tilly waves
>   from Nessa's window and doesn't come.

With this the House deals the house: it can be finished, and it is a Witness source. What waits is listed under "Not yet".

## The rest of the night

- Once the rite is over, either way (Tilly out through the line with her own name, or Tilly answered and back in Nessa's bed), the
  house's hour goes on to first light at eight times the pace of the night.
- The readers at the house at that moment (living, and not watching as spectators) are the ones the morning is for.

## First light

- At first light anybody asleep on the camp bed wakes: "It is getting light. Out in the street, a car is coming."
- Gail's car comes up the street from the end where the House's door is, round the end of the circle, and pulls in at the curb where it
  stood on the first visit ("A car pulls in at the curb", heard forty-eight blocks off).
- Gail gets out at the driver's door, comes round the front of the car and up onto the footpath at the foot of the path. On the first
  visit she drove away; this morning she stays.

## Out: Tilly came out with her own name

- **Callum** is sitting on the front step beside the mat with his shoes on, the first moment nobody sees him or the step.
- **Tilly** is at Gail's side at the curb. If the reader holding her hand has brought her there she lets go of it and stays; otherwise
  she is there the first moment nobody sees her or the place.
- **Gail:** "Morning, love. I came as soon as it was light." Then, to Tilly: "Tilly? Is that you, sweetheart?" **Tilly:** "It's me.
  I'm Tilly."
- **The account** is ready under the mat for each reader the morning is for ("Gail's car is at the curb."; the House's own line names
  the notice in the letterbox if they have not read it). Each keeps an outcome: `brought_tilly_out_with_her_name` (the reader who led
  her through), `saw_tilly_come_out_with_her_name` (near enough to see it) or `there_when_tilly_came_out`.
- **Reading it,** having read the notice, resolves the Pell house for that reader, once (survival); Tilly's spare cane tip is theirs; and
  the house is never dealt to them again. They leave by the street, on foot.
- **A hand on them:** Callum on the step ("Callum is sitting on the step with his shoes on.", "He moves along the step to make room.",
  "He is looking down the path at the car."); Tilly ("It's me. I'm Tilly.", "Is that Gail's car? It sounds like hers.", "I can't hear
  June any more."); Gail ("Morning, love.", "I've got her. Go on.", "Callum's got his shoes on. Look at that.").

## Answered: Tilly answered to Nessa

- **Each reader's ending lands at first light:** "Gail's car is at the curb. Gail is waiting at the foot of the path." Nothing is
  resolved, there is no account and no keepsake, and the house is never dealt to them again.
- **Tilly** is at Nessa's window, waving, with June behind her in her robe, the first moment nobody sees them or the window.
- **Gail:** "June rang in the night. She thinks it's best you come with me, love. Tilly's settled."
- **Going to her** (within three blocks, once she is at the foot of the path) puts a reader in her car: "Gail opens the car door. Up at
  Nessa's window, Tilly is waving. June stands behind her." (or "Gail opens the car door for you." if Tilly is not at the window yet).
  A beat to look back, two and a half seconds; then the black, and in it the car down the street. The reader is set down before the
  House's door, facing it: "The car stops at the end of the street, and Gail lets you out."
- **A reader who does not go:** after a minute and a half, "Out in the street, Gail sounds the horn.", and the same.
- When everybody the morning is for has gone, Gail gets in and the car goes, each the first moment nobody sees it.
- **A hand on them:** Tilly at the window ("I'm staying here with Mum.", "Mum says I can wave.", "Bye."); June ("Go on, love. Gail's
  waiting.", "She's home now.", "Wave bye-bye, Nessa."); Gail ("In you get, love.", "June knows best. She's been doing this a long
  time.", "Tilly's settled. Come on.").

## The cane tip

- An item of its own (Cane Tip), bound to the reader like every keepsake: a small rubber cap, its foot worn flat and pale on one side.
- Its line: "Tilly's spare, worn flat on one side. Carried, you hear a cane find the next door before you do."
- **Carried into an ordinary hall,** a second after the reader comes in, a cane taps twice at a door there that leads on (not the way
  back), heard only by them. Once to a hall.

## The account

"The account: Wren Close", in the reader's words, from their own record, never saying what they are like or how they felt:

- the street and the green lawn, the taped screen door, June taking them in, the rules as June said them;
- Nessa's name at the table and at bedtime, the hairbrush, the nights in the garage, June painting the line, Callum on it;
- what they told Gail on each call, the knife (and the gap after it), June's hand and who it was said to be, what went on their file,
  and the lines in their journal they never wrote;
- the tapes (all of them in order: "by the end I knew what the water was for");
- the last night: the bucket and the gap, the plate for Callum and his name, the pool, Tilly's own name back, her hand, the gap;
- the lanyard, the freezer and Nessa; June brought to her, or seen sitting down;
- first light, Gail's car, Callum on the step with his shoes on, Tilly saying she is Tilly; June still sitting by the freezer, if the
  reader brought her there; the cane tip; and the street: "Behind me nobody was calling any name at all."
