## The offer

In Act II a hand on Vera, wherever she is (in the corridor upstairs, or at Daniel's frame for the pin), gets her offer once:

> Vera: I can mend him. I need help keeping the other one quiet while I do it.
> Vera: It's a matter of making the arrangement stable. It has been loose for years.

## The cost before the help

"The cost is known before anybody can help complete it." Until the reader has heard it, the only replies are questions:

- "And the other one?" (always there);
- "It cut him too." (only for a reader who has seen the rule: either cut, or the pin);
- "Daniel told me what you did to him." (only for a reader who has heard Daniel's account);
- "Say nothing."

Any of the three questions gets the rest of the script:

> Vera: The other one won't come through it. No.
> Vera: He has been cared for. Dan has a life to go back to. Surely you can see what would be wasted.
> Vera: Or you can send Dan back through the frame. The other walks out. It's possible. It's a waste.

The plan's fourth challenge, the cellar, is slice 10's (N9); it is added there.
