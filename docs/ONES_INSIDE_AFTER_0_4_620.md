## What comes back

The lodging house joins the aftermath rooms (`Aftermath.PLACES`, docs/AFTERMATH_0_4_244.md) and comes back **once** to a reader who
read their account of it, never within their last eight crossings, as the Pell house does.

It comes back as **the house they were in, as it ended**: an ended house keeps its chapter, its people where they stopped, and takes
nobody new (0.4.618); the one exception is a reader of the party it ended with (`LodgingEnd.ofParty`), so the scene-copy system puts
a returning reader back into their own house. A reader of that party who left before the end and never read an account also comes
back to it, and finds their account on the bench.

On arrival a finished reader is given one line, by the end the house came to, and the visit is counted as the one return
(`Aftermath.count`). There is nothing to do and nothing to earn. A hand on any of the people gives the same line: nobody there has
anything to ask of them.
