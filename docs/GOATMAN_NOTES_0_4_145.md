# The owner's Goatman notes — 0.4.145

The owner's October 28 notes on the Goatman vignette, after the forward doors.

## The child on the trail

The private child who follows the latecomer up the trail now:

- walks a few steps behind with her head bowed, easing into and out of her walk and never moving faster than a walk;
- does not turn and walk away when the reader turns back toward her. She takes no step and does not turn. She is simply further off, slid back along the trail, still facing the way the reader was going.

She keeps the same distance (2.8 blocks along the trail) and still leaves at the bend as before.

## The shapes in the hollows

A shape standing in a hollow beside the trail is gone the moment anyone looks straight at it, and it does not come back. "Anyone" means any player's camera, spectators included, turned within about sixteen degrees of the shape with nothing solid in between. Glancing past it does not count. As before, it also goes once the reader is past it.

## Bedtime

- **The chat in bed.** While a reader sleeps in the trailer, the chat shown on the bed screen stays at first. Once the Goatman first gets up out of its bunk and stands laughing, it fades out over three seconds and stays hidden while they sleep. Only the chat lines fade; the bed screen's own button and input box stay.
- **The standing laughter** lasts twice as long (seven seconds) and comes four times instead of twice, with the lie-down between, before it crawls to a bed.
- **The crawl** is out of sight the whole way: no body, no eyes, no shadow, for every viewer.
- **The jump.** It springs up visible at the foot of the bed, in front of the sleeper's eyes, with a thump and a laugh. The screen goes black within a second.
- **The end of the night.** Under the black, the reader is woken and carried out of the trailer to the manor: "It was standing at the foot of the bed. Then you were somewhere else." This is the same way opening the door at night already ended the night.
  - Their haunting is kept.
  - Their night is over, with no survival credit.
  - The old morning walk-out no longer follows a bedside visit.
  - Other sleepers get their own visit in turn.
