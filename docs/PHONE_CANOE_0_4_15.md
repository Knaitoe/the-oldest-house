# The Oldest House 0.4.15

NeoForge 1.21.1, Java 21. Install this version on both client and server; the new viewpoint payload advances network protocol to 15.

## Phone in the Canoe

A night jetty overlooks a real submerged steeple. The stars are authored inside the sealed slot, so neighboring House rooms never enter the recording. Water and a wooden hull leak through the closed entrance before the scene is entered.

Board the canoe with an empty main hand and use the scene phone to film. Leaving before filming removes the unused prop and permits a retry. The canoe carries its recorder over the tower for nine seconds. You can look around while recording; frame times, actual coordinates and camera bearings are stored on this original item.

The phone then falls. Its owner's view changes to a separate tracked camera at the waterline, fixed upward toward the night sky for seven seconds. Movement and interactions are held during this short authored clip, and incoming damage cannot trigger a death screen. A blackout returns the living recorder to the jetty with their other possessions intact. Normal view and controls are restored. Other explorers receive neither this player's camera nor their resolution.

The original phone is kept in the existing owner-specific cave canoe record. On a later preserved-cave visit, its owner can retrieve it at the back of the congregation. A full inventory leaves it there for a retry; another player cannot take it. The same custom components, recording identity and frame metadata survive. Use the recovered phone to read the saved frame record in Minecraft's native reader. This artifact exposes a written frame record, rather than a video player. It includes the final upward shot captured after the actual waiting period, not a prewritten future frame.

Scene time pauses offline. Reloads and forced departures preserve a dropped recording; returning and boarding resumes that clip without giving a second phone. An interrupted or merely entered scene awards no resolution. A finished scene exhausts only its recorder, leaving other explorers' personal scenes available. An occupied canoe admits one recorder at a time.

## Witness and existing worlds

Finishing the clip records **one personal memory resolution**. Recovery is not required for that credit and borrowing an artifact does not grant it. The fully playable pool now contains **nine sources**, requiring **seven distinct resolutions** across at least two kinds. The Mother remains optional and there are still **three endings**. Existing evidence, completed endings and releases already underway remain saved.

Builder 16 appends slot 30. Upgrading from builder 15 carves only the new site: school supplies, church locks, cave bodies, visit noise, the old canoe, Shallows participation and all earlier positions remain intact. Older supported incremental upgrades continue to append their missing sites.

## Test in game

```
/oldesthouse door phone_canoe
/oldesthouse vignette phone_canoe status
/oldesthouse door preserved_cave
/oldesthouse finale witness status
```

Walk to the jetty, right-click the canoe with an empty hand, and use the phone. After the blackout, return through the entrance or use the cave test door; inspect its canoe to retrieve your recording. Repeat the test with another player to check personal camera, credit and retrieval ownership. The status command deliberately does not reset or manufacture original recordings.

## Verification

Five new required server GameTests cover real mounted movement, the timed native drop and waterline camera, damage protection without spectator mode or death, authentic frame metadata, inventory preservation, owner-only recovery, readable recording, interruptions and reloads, personal exhaustion, appended slots/leaks and a builder-15 upgrade that retains finite school inventory and the physical cave canoe. Witness tests cover the seven-of-nine threshold and saved accounts. Original audio consists of three mono recordings; all earlier assets are preserved.

The required server suite also remains the regression check for the earlier House systems. The client camera and HUD code compile with the package; visual smoothness and atmosphere still need an in-game client pass.
