# A bar for the sixth: Last orders by copy (0.4.556)

The owner, 13 November: "All multiplayer vignettes should be hooked through that system. If full".

Last orders has been one bar for everyone since 0.4.416: one man in booth four, one match, one pool table, one board, with any number of
readers at the bar. Its evenings were already kept by where the bar stands (an evening, a table and a board for each base), so a second
whole bar has an evening of its own without anything in the story changing. This release gives the bar copies and a number of places.

## Full

| Story | Places | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Last orders | 5 | one to each stool along the bar but Benny's (six stools; Benny has the far one) |

A bar's places are held by the readers in it now. The bar keeps no roll, so a reader who walks out keeps no place; their own ending is
theirs wherever they won it.

- The sixth reader through the door goes into the next bar, built whole a million blocks west, with its own man in booth four, its own
  regulars at their darts, its own Benny, its own table and board and its own evening. Their door waits while it is made ready; it is
  made ready ahead when a bar is down to its last place.
- **The league board and the window are the whole server's**, as they always were: a glass drunk in one bar puts its week on the board
  and turns the street outside to another season in every other bar that stands, where the whole bar is loaded
  (`LastOrders.elsewhere`). Any other bar shows them when its evening next starts.
- The accounts already count company within one copy (0.4.547), so a reader's line says who was in their own bar.

## The case

`LastOrdersTests.aSixthReaderIsGivenABarOfTheirOwn` (literary): five readers in the bar are full to a sixth and never to one of the five.
The sixth's door waits while a second bar is made ready, and one of the five goes back to theirs. The second bar stands a million blocks
west and the sixth goes in by its own door. Each bar has its own man in booth four and its own evening. A glass the sixth drinks in the
second bar puts its week up under their name on both bars' league boards.

## Decided here (any of it can move)

- Five places: the stools but Benny's.
- The league board and the window stay the whole server's, shown in every bar.

## Not yet

- **The séance, Camp Blood, the lake nights (Movie Night, Costume Night, Winter Lake) and the hotel**: each keeps its story in one record
  for the whole world, so a copy cannot have a night of its own until that record is kept by copy.
