# The six uncanny rooms against their texts (0.4.116)

The owner asked for the six rooms of 0.4.113 to be weighed against the concepts and found texts they came from, and revised
where they fell short. The found texts and both artifact tags are already the owner's words exactly (checked against the
supplied logs; `foundTextsKeepEveryWordAcrossReadableNativePages` keeps them so). The changes are to what the rooms do.

## Boarded House

The Propped already vanish in the middle of the view and stand only at its edges, and they close in over time.
*"Until you back into a corner where your FOV can no longer push them away"* did not work: they held at 1.15 blocks and
the catch needed 1.05, so nobody was ever caught. Now a reader with a wall on one side and another at right angles to it
(a doorway's two jambs are not a corner) has nowhere left to push them: they close to a block, and within a block and a half
the room takes the reader. Away from corners nothing changes.

## Blue-Light Diner

*"Don't answer her when she asks how your meal is."* When she sets the meal down she asks "How's your meal?", and again
once her mouth has split. Answering her (using her, as one would to speak) clears that reader's table: the room's failure,
for them alone. Touching her before she has asked is nothing. The frost is black, as on the receipt: dark specks at booth
four, black edges on the screen, "Butter knives rattle. Black frost by booth four."

## Stooping Giant

As written: she freezes on green and walks on red, at her own lazy pace if the reader stands still and at theirs if they
move ("If you sprint, you just give her something to pace"), so ground is gained only on green. Unchanged. The wall signs
now do what the clipboard says: detour warnings first, then flickering on and off with the count of steps left before she
reaches the reader.

## Split-Feed Bedroom

Nothing in the notebook asks for a second bed within ninety seconds, and missing it failed the room for good. Now an
unfinished first night simply lapses: the room unlocks and the bed is still there. The second night's marionette tiptoes
(about fifty seconds to the parents' door rather than twenty-five), leaving time to see the feeds, look with one's own
eyes and shut the door. Waking from the first night: "06:00. Dried mud between your toes."; from the second: "06:00. On
the monitor, the mattress is empty."

## Unbuttoned Basement

*"Stop for five minutes to catch your breath and he lays down three new coats."* He now grows only while nobody is peeling:
a fold comes every six seconds unless someone peeled in the last five; the worker's blade holds him at first and his nails
hold every other fold after it snaps. Folds never close inside a living body: a reader the next fold would cover is taken
instead (*"knitted him straight into its hip"*), by the room's own failure, set down upstairs, rather than suffocating in
solid blocks. The cellar's clock was shared and never reset, so one long visit left it solid for every later reader and
let them claim the "blade broke" ending without waiting. Once nobody has worked the cellar for ten seconds, the next reader
finds its night beginning again: the grown folds gone, the man and his key's cavity as authored, the worker's worn blade
back in his hand. Readers' own peeling, keys and accounts are untouched.

## Buzzer at the Threshold

*"Do not touch the brass button"* is written beside it; before that has been read the button admits nothing. *"If the
light goes out in front of you, and the unhinged door clicks open behind you"*: in the dark, an unhinged frame the reader is
not facing now clicks open by itself (a latch sound and "A latch clicks behind you."), and backing into it loops the hall
as before. It shuts when the floodlight comes back on. A frame opened by hand stays open.

## Artifacts

As tagged: the bone ring outlines nearby people through walls with a one-word intent above them; the smoky mirror takes two
health and shows what stands in the corners (the Propped, the waitress before she is solid, the unhinged frames, the
marionette on the feeds). Unchanged.

## Tests

Two new uncanny cases (answering her; the folds) and extended board, sleep, buzzer and floodlight cases: the corner rule is
covered by the existing board case's geometry; a lapsed first night unlocks without failing; the unread button admits
nothing; a frame behind the reader opens in the dark and shuts with the light; a fold is held while someone peels, takes
the reader without filling their space, and the empty cellar resets for a latecomer. No PNG, protocol, layout or Witness
change: 52 sources / 39 required / two kinds / three endings.
