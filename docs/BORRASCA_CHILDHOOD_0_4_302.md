# Nell's games and the older names

Nell leads off with tag and hide-and-seek near Drisking. The compass is part of both games. The player can follow her directly to the Triple Tree if they head uphill first. Eli and Wren stand along the trail and have further things to say when spoken to again.

The fort is a playhouse before it is a place to tell the legend. The ridge can be heard before Nell explains it. She tells the reader what a name on the tree means and asks them to follow her down. The older cuts are visible before either child adds a name. Nell has more things to say if the reader stays with her on either childhood visit.

During the taking, a worker carries Nell along the ridge route. The workers warn a reader who follows them uphill and send them back through their entry under a fade if they persist. Nell and the workers use the Ghosts cast's expression geometry adapted to their own skins. The workers' footfalls vary with the surface, and the sheriff lowers his hat-tipping arm after speaking.
