# Neighbours: the House beside other mods

The House shares its world with other mods without depending on any of them. The first is *A Reaping* (mod id `a_reaping`), a Hunger Games mod whose players live in districts in the Overworld and fight in an arena dimension. The design both mods follow is A Reaping's `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-house-compatibility-design.md`, and its evidence, with a file and line for every House site, is `2026-11-17-house-compatibility-research.md` beside it. It came in 0.4.675 (`docs/NEIGHBOURS_0_4_675.md`, which also says what it does not cover).

The owner, on the district: "The house should require the player to sneak out from the district. Having Hillary and the like spawn inside would likely still require the player to sneak out, while adding another layer of convolution."

## How they speak

Nothing in the House names another mod's classes, and nothing in a neighbour names the House's. They speak through:

- **Tags.** The mod that reads a tag declares it as an empty file; the other fills it from its own jar.
- **Inter-mod messages** (`InterModComms`), sent in `InterModEnqueueEvent` and read in `InterModProcessEvent`, whose payloads are `java.util.function` types only. A message to a mod that is not loaded is dropped.
- **Scoreboard tags.**

`house/Neighbours` holds the House's half. Without a neighbour every tag is empty and no message comes, and the House is as it was.

| What | Kind | Direction | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#the_oldest_house:not_home` | block tag, declared empty here | a neighbour fills it | a bed that counts for sleep and never for home (A Reaping's issued beds) |
| `#the_oldest_house:not_own_world` | dimension type tag, declared empty here | a neighbour fills it | a world that is not the reader's own (A Reaping's arena, later its Capitol and train): the House keeps out of it |
| `district_land` | message, `BiPredicate<ServerLevel, BlockPos>` | a neighbour sends it; any number, combined with OR | true on a district's land and worksites and at least 8 blocks round them (A Reaping's margin). The House probes a box for it only every 2 blocks, so a predicate that held on a narrower strip, a road or one worksite block, could pass between two probes |
| `house_land` | message, `BiPredicate<ServerLevel, BlockPos>` | the House sends it to `a_reaping` | true on the manor's lot in the Overworld (`HouseLayout` MIN..MAX in x and z round its origin, at any height) and 8 blocks round it, from the morning the House comes, standing or fallen |
| `#the_oldest_house:left_alone` | entity type tag, declared empty here | a neighbour fills it | a neighbour's own people (A Reaping's sparring partner, later its tributes, guards, citizens and kin): never projected by the mirror, killed by Harrigan's call, counted by the tally counter or copied into a copied home |
| `#the_oldest_house:hunted` | entity type tag, declared empty here | a neighbour fills it | a neighbour's game animals: game to the elk's men, as the animals of the reader's world are |
| `#a_reaping:sheltered` | dimension type tag, filled here | A Reaping reads it | the House's own dimension types, `house_interior`, `outside` and `between`: A Reaping's fighting and body rules pause there |
| `#a_reaping:not_fighters` | entity type tag, filled here | A Reaping reads it | every living entity type of the House's: never a target, an ambush or someone aware |
| `house_made` | message, `Predicate<ItemStack>` | the House sends it to `a_reaping` | the House's own things (`HouseKeeping.houseMade`): its own items, and anything with custom data holding more than the House's stamps |
| `a_reaping.not_a_fighter` | scoreboard tag | the House puts it on Hillary | she is never fought while she guides; she is a vanilla wolf, and no type tag can tell her from any other |

A predicate that throws is taken as not holding and said once in the log. A neighbour's `district_land` is handed a position of its own, which it may keep: never a cursor the House moves on. The cases install a district without a neighbour through `Neighbours.installDistrictForTesting`, and take it away in a `finally` or an `@AfterBatch` hook.

## R1. Home is a bed of the reader's own, outside every district

`opening/HomeBed`.

**A home bed** meets all four:
1. It is in the Overworld.
2. It is a bed (`#minecraft:beds`), when its chunk is loaded.
3. It is not in `#the_oldest_house:not_home`.
4. No neighbour's `district_land` holds at it.

**The reader's home** is the first of:
1. their respawn, if it is a home bed;
2. the home bed they last woke in on a morning, which the opening remembers in its player state (`OpeningPlayerState.Home`: the bed, the House's day it became home, the nights slept in it);
3. none.

An unloaded bed is trusted, as the respawn always was: it can only have been set or remembered by using one. A remembered bed the House sees gone (its chunk loaded and no bed there) is forgotten, so it is not trusted again when its chunk unloads; one on a district's land or in `#not_home` is only passed over, since those can change back.

**What counts where.**
- The opening waits for a home: a reader who only ever sleeps in a district never starts it.
- The opening's nights count only a night in a home bed or in one of the manor's. A morning in a home bed is what the House remembers.
- Sleep still counts everywhere for the House's own clock and for what ends with any night's sleep: its age, the co-author's note, lateness, the Line.
- The key tag's feeding and the front-door key's mending come only with a wake in a home bed or a manor bed.
- A door's use is counted only near home, and only when nothing else refused the click (the count listens last).
- Nothing the opening sets down, the letter, the journal and Hillary, lands on district land, and no door there is taken for their front door. When the door they use most stands off the district but its step is on it, the nearest other door is tried before the floor beside the bed.

**Every home read takes the home**, and its own no-home branch when there is none:

## R2. The House never edits district land

`house/HouseSpawnManager`, `house/HouseLayout`, `labyrinth/ElkFollows`, `labyrinth/OwnLight`, `labyrinth/FinaleController`, `labyrinth/HotelVignette`.

**The manor's site.**
- No site lies within 8 blocks (`Neighbours.MARGIN`) of a district. The design's envelope (`HouseLayout.ENVELOPE`: MIN..MAX in x and z, from 13 below the origin, where the foundations under the floor course end, to MAX_Y) is widened by 8 on every side and probed every 2 blocks and on its far faces; a district's land comes with a margin of its own, wider than that. The envelope is the design's number, not the House's whole reach: under the cellar and the lowest front step the supports start a row under the lot and run down to 18 below the origin (`HouseLayout.LOWEST_SUPPORT_Y`), so a district under those is kept three blocks off, not eight. Districts are surface land. A neighbour is asked last, and only of a site that would be chosen. The search's report counts the sites passed over for a district.
- Nothing built may stand anywhere on the lot (`HouseLayout.SITE`, MIN..MAX in x, y and z), nor under it where the House would reach it, which closes two blind spots the search had.
  - It looked only from the highest ground up, so a low wall, a fence, a shed or tilled ground lower down was buried under the floor and cut by the cellar. It now looks down to the cellar's floor in every column, and on under it as far as a foundation or a support would run down through air, plants that give way and water (`HouseLayout.SUPPORT_RUN`, 12 rows below its first), and at what that run would stop on: the run buries it, and tilled ground under it turns to dirt. So a fence at the foot of a dip as deep as any pass takes (5 in the open pass, 8 wooded, 12 rough), at the bottom of a pond, or on the floor of a cave under the cellar blocks the site. The search follows such a run down from a row under the lot in every column, where the cellar's foundations, the lowest step's support and the jetty's ladder start, so it may pass over a site for something only a run from there would reach; something sealed in the ground under the floor, where no run gets to, leaves the site be.
  - It looked only at the cleared ground, from three blocks in front of the house, while the steps come down and the demolition reaches eight; it now looks at the whole lot.
  - Only air, the ground as the world made it (stone, dirt, sand, gravel, clay, terracotta, snow and ice, ores, bedrock, water and lava), what the world grows or leaves in swamps, lush caves, geodes and the sea (lily pads, mangrove roots, cave vines, dripleaf, amethyst, kelp, sea pickles and coral) and growth (`isNaturalGrowth`) may be there. None of these is cleared for the House; they are only not taken for something built.
  - Three things the world leaves only in some places, and a player can set anywhere, are the ground only there (`naturalOnTheLot`): fossil bone in the ground under the lot, where a run would find it in a cave; the mossy cobblestone of a forest rock (old-growth taiga) below the origin, where the floor buries it; and a bees' nest hanging over the ground, as the trees of meadows and flower forests carry them where the lot reaches out in front. Anywhere else each is taken for something built, so a player's bone-block path, mossy wall or moved nest is never buried or cleared. The trade-off, kept on purpose: a fossil that reaches up into the lot, a forest rock standing at or above the origin in front of the cleared ground (in the cleared ground one always turned the site down), and a nest sitting on the ground turn the site down.
  - The whole lot is read only of a site that would be chosen, after its water is counted, as the neighbour is asked.
- The finale's demolition clears `HouseLayout.SITE`, as it always did; the search now checks the box it clears. It reaches no lower than the cellar's floor: the foundations and supports under it stay, and so does the last row of the jetty's ladder, which can reach one row under the lot.

**The other edits.**
- The elk never takes a penned animal standing on a district, nor opens a gate on one. With no other animal she takes nothing, and the debt is kept. The gate she opens is the taken animal's own, the nearest; when it is on a district it stays shut and no other pen's gate is opened instead.
- Your Own Light's visit touches nothing on a district: no chest, candle or campfire there. The chest turned round is the nearest the reader opened themselves (the House keeps the last sixteen chests a reader opened within 16 blocks of home, `Opened213`, those opened before they were dealt the story included; opening the newest again writes nothing), else the first found, as it always was.
- The endings' chest stands in the first free cell off any district; with no free cell, district or not, the pages are left for the reader as delivered items at home, where before they waited for a free cell forever. A reader with no home is left them where they stand in the Overworld, and only once they stand where pages can lie: nothing flowing there and solid ground under it. Swimming, riding a boat, gliding, falling, or over lava or a cactus, they wait, since pages left there could be lost before they are taken. The sixty mornings' overgrowth leaves every cell on a district as it was, and plants no vine next to one, above or below it, where the vine could spread onto it in a step. A vine further off could still creep there in time, but a district's land comes with a margin of its own to cross first.
- The hotel tab is never collected from a container on a district: a chest, barrel or cart where it stands, either half of a double chest, and a container that stands nowhere of its own (an ender chest's contents) where the reader stands.

Without a neighbour no district holds anywhere, and only the two blind spots (the search now checks the demolition's box, which is unchanged, and what the foundations and supports reach under the lot), the pages that waited for a free cell and Own Light's preference for a chest the reader opened are new.

## R3. The Games are not the reader's own world

`house/HouseDimensions.ownWorld`, `house/Neighbours.notOwnWorld`.

**The reader's own world** is anywhere that is none of the House's dimensions and whose dimension type is not in `#the_oldest_house:not_own_world`. Every House test that took "not the House" for "their own world" now asks this. The tag is read on both sides: the game sends dimension type tags to every client, so the client's own-world reads (the note's grip on ice and snow, the helmet's muffled sound) agree with the server's. Without a neighbour the tag is empty and the reader's own world is anywhere outside the House, as it was.

**What keeps out of a world in the tag:**
- **The elk** (`ElkFollows`): the meat and hide do not spoil there, nothing killed there is owed in kind, a young one there costs no night, and her field never finds them there. The morning in kind waits while they are there, and comes at their first dawn back, so no word of a taken animal reaches them there. Where the field found a reader is kept with its world and let go when they change worlds (`PlayerChangedDimensionEvent`), so a restless reader taken into another world at night is never brought back to their Overworld x and z there; that is new in every world.
- **The Lake House lamp** (`LakeHouseLightBlock`): a death there does not arm its respawn.
- **The hotel tab** (`HotelVignette`): no chest opened there collects anything.
- **The storeroom** (`Storeroom.returnTo`): the pocket return waits while they are there, and finds them once they are out. In the House it comes as it always did.
- **Weapon history** (`WeaponHistory.watched`): no blow or bow drawn there is recorded (a weapon let fall is no use anywhere, R7), and nothing a reader looses there stamps the weapon it came from or carries the original's mark (`FinaleController.projectile`), so the House writes nothing on a neighbour's bow there.
- **The prizes** (`PrizeWear`, `PolybiusPrize`): nothing comes off into the hand there, and the mood ring shows nothing: violet, however its wearer is, so nobody there reads their wounds off it.
- **Lateness** (`OwnLightLate`): a late reader is not drawn late there. It waits for them, since no bed there ends it, and they are drawn late again once they are out. Everyone is told on the tick they go in and on the tick they come out (`PlayerChangedDimensionEvent`), not at the next second's sync, so nobody there is drawn a beat behind for a moment after they arrive.
- **The Goatman** (`GoatmanHaunt.hauntsHere`): it is not with them there; it neither eats with them nor is glimpsed, and eats with them again once they are out.
- **The keepsakes and artifacts** (`Keepsakes.ownWorld`, `Artifacts.ownWorld`): everything that works "out in your world" does nothing there, the scrawled note's doors included, and the kept bike is not ridden ("Not here.", where the House's halls say "Not in here."). The children's chalk is the House's and asks for the House itself, so it does nothing there either.
- **Others found that took "not the House" for "their own world":** Bly's Lady never takes their arm there, and a carry under way when they are taken there is let go on her next step, her dark and her hold lifting with it (only her own: hidden, and no longer than she gives); taken into any other world, she walks them on to the carry's x and z there, as she always has; the film (`WorkPrint.followsHere`) does not follow them there; Harrigan's line has no service there, so no call or text goes and nothing rings ("No service. The clock still keeps the time back home."), and a night ring begun in their own world before they were taken there rings no more once they are there: it is a missed call in the thread, without a word, for them to find when they open the phone (in the House a ring begun outside rings on, as it always has); the envelope neither seals, opens nor returns there ("Nothing happens."), and a sealed one falls with its keeper there; the Lake House flashlight lights nothing and the helmet gives no air and muffles nothing there; the stela's stone parts nothing there ("Nothing here gives to it."); the Goatman's counter clicks and counts nothing there ("It clicks, but the number doesn't turn here."), and keeps no watch over a sleeping keeper there; the church key keeps no drowned off its bearer there, as the essay page keeps none off (the key still counts the House's outside dimension as outside, as it always did); and the séance's planchette points at nothing that stalks them there.

**Not Yet** (`NotYetMending`): a reader taken out of the ward's world while the machine mends them (by a neighbour's Games, a command, anything but the machine) is finished at once where they are, as one who goes is: the seam, the cot and the counter, the hold let go and the dark lifted. The cot is theirs from then on, as it is for one who wakes on it, so once they have the rule the machine has a cot to light and keep them on. They are never laid on a table or a cot in the world they are in. One taken off the table is got up, since the game's move between worlds leaves a sleeper lying, and the arms over the table fold unless another reader is on it. That is new in every world.

**What works in every world is left to the strip.** The Uncanny bone ring in the off hand and a woken smoky mirror list every human shape within 14 blocks with its intent, and the Usher stone knocks from the nearest unseen creature within 32. They work in the House and out of it alike, so they are not own-world tests and are not changed here, but in the Games they would tell their holder where hidden bodies are, through walls. A Reaping's strip (the design's R4) empties every pocket before the Games and keeps the House's things (`house_made`) for their owner, so they do not go in. Until the strip exists, a reader who carries them in has them there. Whether the House should keep them out itself is the owner's call.

**New writing.** One line, for Harrigan's phone opened where there is no service and it is not the House: "No service. The clock still keeps the time back home." One for the Goatman's counter clicked where it keeps no count: "It clicks, but the number doesn't turn here." One for the kept bike there, where "Not in here." would be the House's indoors: "Not here." And the lines that promised an effect "away from the House" or "outside" now say the House's own phrase for the reader's own world, as the artifacts' lines already did, so they promise nothing in the Games; without a neighbour they mean what they meant:

Only if it goes with him may your own phone reach his number out in your world." and "Your own phone can reach his number now. Out in your world, open it to call him, or text him a creature's name."

They are here to be rewritten on the Text Review page.

## R5. The House is sheltered from a neighbour's fighting

The House's half is two tag files and a scoreboard tag; the pausing is A Reaping's.

- `data/a_reaping/tags/dimension_type/sheltered.json` lists the House's three dimension types.
- `data/a_reaping/tags/entity_type/not_fighters.json` lists every living entity type the House registers. `LeftAloneTests` makes one of every type in the House's namespace and fails, by name, for a living one the file leaves out or a list entry that is not living, so a new person or creature cannot be forgotten.
- **Hillary** (`Hillary.guiding`) carries `a_reaping.not_a_fighter` from her reader's doorstep until her introduction to the manor is done (or, with her reader online, until they have been inside, as her walk to the manor stops then), and in the finale while she is its guide; never as one of the Mother's pets. While she leads she has no owner, so a neighbour's rule for a body's own animals cannot cover her. It comes off after, on her next tick.

## R7. What the House made

`house/HouseKeeping`, `labyrinth/WeaponHistory`, `labyrinth/MinotaurEntity`.

- **House-made** (`HouseKeeping.houseMade`) is the House's own items, and anything carrying custom data with something in it besides the House's own stamps. The stamps are a weapon's identity and the mark of a House copy (`HouseOriginalWeapon`, `HouseWeaponCopy`) and the Mother's marks of who has carried a thing and since when (`the_oldest_house_mother_carrier`, `the_oldest_house_mother_carried`): the House writes them on outside things too, and before this the Mother's mark on every unstackable thing carried for ten seconds let any outside pickaxe, shears or armour walk past the hall drawers. Now such a thing is put away like anything else from outside. The Mother herself is not changed. The House tells A Reaping this predicate as `house_made`; it does not include the favourite below, which is the reader's own weapon and passes only the drawers.
- **The favourite passes** (the owner, 17 November: "Yes, to favorite weapon"). The reader's current favourite (`WeaponHistory.favorite`, the stamped weapon the finale would arm them with) passes the door and the hall, and no other stamped thing: not a House copy of it, and not a weapon that was the favourite before another was used more. It is kept as the reader's own thing (loose): carried out, it is not taken for something the House gave them, and once it is no longer the favourite it goes to the drawer at the hall like their other things. Set down in the manor, it is followed like the reader's other things there: picked up past the hall by its own reader while it is still their favourite, it stays with them as their own; picked up by another reader, or once it is no longer the favourite, it is like anything else of theirs set down there (loose in the hands of whoever picks it up in the manor, back to its owner's drawer if picked up past the hall), so another reader never carries it past the hall. The favourite is only what the House has counted: asking for it stamps and counts nothing. The once-only seeding of older saves from the game's item statistics (`WeaponHistory.seed`), which the first ask would have run at the door, is gone (new builds only), and so is the last of it: a weapon with no identity let fall in a world of the reader's own was recorded with the game's count of uses of its kind (`Stats.ITEM_USED`, which counts every block mined with it), so a never-swung iron axe dropped once by a reader who had chopped with iron axes became the favourite and passed the hall while the sword they fought with went to the drawer. Letting a weapon fall is no use, and the House never reads the game's item statistics for weapons. A weapon never used in a fight is nobody's favourite, at the door, the Old Cabin, the copied home or the finale.
- **A stamp never changes whose a thing is.** The lists the drawers keep (the drawer, loose, carried out) match a thing whatever the House has stamped on it since it was listed: the Mother's marks always, and a weapon's identity unless both carry one. So an outside pickaxe taken out of the drawer and marked by the Mother, or an outside sword swung once in the manor, still goes back at the hall, and something the House gave, marked or swung outside, still comes back in. The House looks for an exact match first, so an outside sword with no identity yet never takes the place of the House's own sword that has one.
- **Another mod's weapon** is a weapon to the House when it is in NeoForge's common tags `#c:tools/melee_weapon` or `#c:tools/ranged_weapon`, so it can be the favourite and be carried to the Minotaur. A use of it is counted by the blow it deals (`LivingDamageEvent.Post`): a blow whose damage source is the reader's and whose weapon is such a weapon, once a tick for each weapon however many it hurts (another mod's sweep, a thrust through two, a volley), as a vanilla sweep is one attack. A melee blow's source carries the main hand; a projectile's carries the weapon that loosed it, a copy, so the use is the real one's in the reader's hand. A weapon is stamped as its first projectile joins the world, after the projectile took its copy, so a copy with no identity is matched to the hand with the House's stamps set aside, and the first shot of a mod's bow is a use like the rest. A vanilla attack is counted only if it goes through: the House hears it last, so one another listener cancels (A Reaping's weapons take the swing for their own moves) is no use, and its own blow is counted when it lands. The damage of a vanilla attack, its sweep included, was counted as the attack (that tick) and is not counted again; a vanilla bow, crossbow or trident is counted when drawn or thrown, as before, and not again when what it loosed hits; thorns, which the reader's armour deals, are no use. Nothing is counted in a world in `#not_own_world`, as before.
- **The Minotaur's "only the original"** also knows the original by what dealt the blow: a projectile whose weapon (`DamageSource.getWeaponItem`) is the stamped original wounds it though the House put no mark on the projectile, as with another mod's projectile carrying its bow, and so does a blow whose source names the original.

Without a neighbour no other mod's weapon is in the common tags unless some mod put it there; what changes in every world is that the House's stamps alone let nothing past the drawers, that the favourite does, and that the Minotaur reads the damage source.

## R9. A neighbour's people are left alone

`house/HouseExteriorEntityMirror`, `labyrinth/HarriganVignette`, `labyrinth/TallyCounterItem`, `labyrinth/LiteraryCopies`.

- **The mirror** never projects a type in `#left_alone`, either way across the manor's seam, and what it copies of anyone it does project carries none of their own NeoForge data (`NeoForgeData`, the House's or a neighbour's), as it already carried none of their attachments. A projection is scenery: without a neighbour, only the stripped data is new.
- **Harrigan's call** refuses a type in `#left_alone`, by kind or by name, as it refuses the House's own and the bosses, and the morning's sweep after a call (`HarriganVignette.sweeps`) asks again, so a kind listed between the call and the morning is not taken.
- **The tally counter** counts no type in `#left_alone` among what is out there, and nothing of one wakes a sleeping keeper or clicks behind one.

- **The copied homes** (`LiteraryCopies.captures`) take the animals, frames and stands round a home bed and never a type in `#left_alone`, and what they keep of what they take (`LiteraryCopies.captured`) carries no NeoForge data and no attachments, the House's or a neighbour's, as the mirror's projections carry none. That is new in every world: a copy in the House was already a frozen projection, and now it can no longer carry the House's own marks (a guide's, Hillary's) with it.
