# The Pell house 12: the knife (0.4.534)

The owner, 12 November: "start by finishing each unfinished vignette". The Pell house (`docs/PELL_HOUSE_PROPOSAL.md`) has its first
visit whole and the start of its second, a week in (`docs/PELL_HOUSE_WEEK_0_4_533.md`). This is its twelfth slice, the knife, from the
proposal's "2. A week in":

> **The fight:** at night the older sibling finds Callum in the kitchen with the knife from the empty slot, and they fight him for it.
> **The cut:** a fist lunges, then a sharp cut to black. **After the cut:** static fills the reader's own view while their breathing,
> heavy at first, settles as the static clears. **Afterwards:** the knife is gone, there's a towel on the floor, and June is in the
> doorway. **The next morning,** Gail has June's version: the older sibling went for Callum. **Never shown:** what happened in the
> black, and no wound on anyone.

and the first of Gail's marks, from "Being framed": "Each framed incident ... comes to Gail as June's version first", and "Her file ...
gains a mark for each incident the reader can't answer." June's hand, the tapes, the freezer and the journal's false entries come after.
The House still does not deal the house.

## One o'clock

- **A week in, in the small hours**, once June is back in her room from the line: at one, the first moment nobody sees Callum, the sink,
  his drawer or the sliding door, he is at the kitchen sink where June washes up, the knife from his drawer in his hand. The light over
  the sink is on, and the sliding door stands open, unlocked: it is how he came in from the line.
- **Nobody sees** means nobody awake, spectators too, within 48 blocks and looking that way, with nothing in the way but the house's
  clear glass or the pool fence's bars. A reader at the kitchen's window sees the sink.
- **Whoever is asleep on the camp bed wakes**: "Something wakes you. Over at the house, a light is on in the kitchen."
- **The hour holds** from then until June is back in her room after it, unless every reader at the house is asleep.
- **If he cannot get there unseen before first light**, there is no knife that night.

## In the kitchen

- **A reader who comes into the kitchen** is told, once: "Callum is at the sink with his back to the room. There is a knife in his
  hand."
- **After a moment** he looks round at whoever in the kitchen is nearest to him, and keeps looking at them.
- **A hand on him** takes his wrist ("You take hold of his wrist."), and they struggle for it. A reader who stands close to him (within
  1.8 blocks) for 50 ticks, he turns on, and they struggle too.
- **The struggle** lasts 50 ticks: the two of them pulling, a scuffle of feet and cloth and a chair knocked, him turned to whoever has
  hold of him. A hand on him then: "He pulls against you. He is stronger than he looks."
- **Then his fist comes** at their face (a sweep of the arm, 6 ticks), and the cut.

## The cut

- **Black**, at once and complete, for everyone in the kitchen or within four blocks of him, and for anyone awake who could see him at
  that moment, through the window or the arch. It holds for 60 ticks.
- **Then static** over the reader's own view, thick at first and clearing over 120 ticks, while the black lifts from under it in the
  first quarter. The black is the story's: it always shows. The static follows the screen-effects setting (Comfort), down to none.
- **Their breathing**, for each reader who was in it with him: four breaths as the static clears, heavy and fast at first, then
  slower and quieter.
- **In the black**, Callum is back on his mattress in his room, asleep, with nothing in his hand. The knife is gone. A tea towel lies
  on the kitchen floor where nobody is standing ("A tea towel on the kitchen floor, folded over on itself."), and June is in the
  kitchen's arch, her own door open behind her.
- **Never shown:** what happened in the black. Nobody is hurt.
- **Who June found** is whoever was in the kitchen or with him when it went black. A reader who only looked in sees it go black and is
  not among them.

## After

- **Two seconds after the black** June, from the arch, turned to whoever is nearest: "Go on back to bed, love." A hand on her while she
  is up after it: "Bed, love. We'll talk in the morning."
- **Once the static has cleared** she turns the light off, goes to the sliding door, and asks anyone in the house out ("Out you go, love.
  I'm locking up."). Once nobody is inside or in the doorway she shuts it and locks it, goes back to her room and shuts her door. The
  night goes on.
- **Callum sleeps on** in his room. He does not go back out to the line that night, and is at breakfast.
- **The house after:** the knife is not back in his drawer ("...spoons and forks in rows, and a space between them where something
  longer lay."), and the block still has its one empty slot.
- **At six,** on her way to lay breakfast, June picks the towel up off the kitchen floor, and the light is off.
- **At breakfast,** once the goggles have been asked after, June has a word for the table: "Knives stay in the block in this house,
  love." Heard by the readers near enough to her.

## Nobody comes

If nobody has come into the house a minute after he is at the sink, or every reader has gone back to sleep, then the first moment nobody
sees him, his drawer, the line or the sliding door, the knife is back in his drawer and he is out on the line again, the light off and the
sliding door shut and locked as June left it, and no towel on the floor. It is as if it never was: nobody was found and nobody is on
Gail's file. The night goes on.

## Gail's file

- **Each reader June found** goes on Gail's file once for the knife ("GailMarks", "MarkKnife"). Nobody else does.
- **Whatever they say.** The proposal's mark is "for each incident the reader can't answer", and nothing in the house yet answers one:
  the things that clear a mark (the transcript page, the "DO NOT SWITCH OFF" note, a tape played while Gail is in the room) come in
  later slices. So for now a reader June found is on the file whatever they tell Gail. What three marks do comes later too.

## The week's call, after the knife

  girl." / "Hang on, I'll put them on."

## How it is made

- **`PellKnife`** has the night: `tick` (from `PellMorning`'s small hours, before the swim; true while the knife has the house), its
  stages (`NOT_YET`, `AT_SINK`, `STRUGGLE`, `LUNGE`, `BLACK`, `AFTER`, `LOCKING`, `OVER`, and `QUIET` when nobody came), its timings
  (`WAITS` 1200, `COMES_AT` 50, `STRUGGLE_FOR` 50, `LUNGE_FOR` 6, `BLACK_FOR` 60, `CLEAR_FOR` 120, `SPEAKS` 40), `CLOSE` 1.8 and `WITH`
  4, Callum's spot at the sink (`SINK`) and June's in the arch (`ARCH`), her ways to the sliding door and back to her room, and a hand on
  Callum (`PellPeople.talk` asks it first).
- **The household** keeps where the knife has got to (`knife`), when (`knifeAt`), who has hold of him (`fighter`), how long a reader
  has stood close (`closeSince`), whom June found (`found`), and her steps after it (`knifeSaid`, `knifeWay`). `PellVisit.clock` holds
  the hour while `PellKnife.holds`; `PellNight.callum` leaves him alone while the knife has him; `PellVisit.restart` undoes it all.
- **The kitchen:** the light over the sink is a light block (level 12) at `LIGHT`, on and off; the towel is the `TOWEL` thing's stage
  1, laid in the first of `TOWELS` nobody stands in; his drawer is the `CUTLERY` thing's stage 1 (the knife) and 2 (its space).
- **The knife** is the item `pell_knife`, drawn by `tools/draw_pell_people.py` (a steel blade with a lit spine and edge, a bolster, a
  black handle with two rivets). Callum has two new gestures, `STRUGGLE` (both arms forward and jerking, his head down) and `LUNGE` (his
  right arm thrown up and over, the left back).
- **The cut** is a payload (`PellCutPayload`, the black and the clearing in ticks; protocol 186), drawn over everything by
  `PellCutClient`: a full black while it holds, then three-pixel static cells with bands that shift, thinning as it clears, scaled by
  Comfort. The breaths are the well's breath (`NovelRegistry.WELL_BREATH`), sent to each reader who was in it, louder and higher first.
  The scuffle and the sweep are the game's own sounds. No new sound.
- **The call:** `PellCall.knife` (a week in, with somebody found) chooses June's side, Gail's lines and the answers on offer, and
  `PellTexts.gailKnife` Gail's answers.
- **For tests:** `PellKnife.beforeOne` (the small hours, just before one), `PellKnife.skip` (a night on which nobody came), and
  `PellKnife.foundThere` (the night as the knife leaves it, these readers found).
