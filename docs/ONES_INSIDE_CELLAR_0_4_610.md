# The Ones Already Inside 10: the cellar (0.4.610)

The plan's tenth slice (`docs/THE_ONES_ALREADY_INSIDE_PLAN.md`): "N9: the cellar, the dead post, the third way learned." The House still
does not deal the house.

New builds only. No layout, protocol or Witness change. One new shoe state (the wedge knocked out, lying on the flags, drawn by
`tools/draw_lodging_house.py`) and four new poses for the body at the dead post. The logic is `LodgingCellar`.

## The wedge

The dead post in the cellar's corner stands in its own iron shoe with a wedge driven in beside it. A hand on the post or its shoe says what
it is, and that the wedge is loose enough to kick out. A crouched hand kicks it out:

> The wedge skids out across the flags. The post shifts in its shoe, and the body tied to it lets out a breath.

The wedge lies by the shoe ("It would go back in with a kick."). A crouched hand drives it back:

> The wedge goes back in under the heel. The post settles. The body tied to it is still.

The wedge and the post are the house's: whoever kicks it out, it is out for everyone; whoever drives it back, it is back for everyone.

## The body at the post

With the wedge out, the failed body tied sitting at the post's foot comes loose at the joins, one stage every seven seconds of the
house's clock (`LOOSEN`, 140 ticks), four stages, and no further. It trembles through every stage.

1. Its right wrist slips the rope.
2. That arm drops away from the shoulder, a gap opening there; the head goes over to one side and down from the neck.
3. The left arm slides off the post sideways and down, its shoulder opening too; the head lower, a leg turned.
4. Every gap wider; the body leaning.

It stays awake. Every six seconds, while any reader is within earshot (18 blocks), it says the script's line:

> The failed body: Put it back. Put it back.

The wedge driven back, it is tied as it was and still.

## The third way

A reader who sees it come apart, from the second stage on (within ten blocks, nothing between), is told once what it means:

> It is coming apart where the post held it together. Every post down here holds up a room, and the ratchet holds every post. Lift the
> pawl and slip the catch of a frame with somebody in it, and all of it lets go at once: whoever was taken walks out, and whoever was made
> to stand in their place comes apart like this. Dan with them.

From then on that reader knows the third way (KnowsThird0610). Doing it (the ratchet and the frame's catch) is E3's, a later slice. A
reader upstairs, or one who kicked the wedge and left before the arm fell, has not seen it and does not know.

## Vera

A reader who knows the third way has, with Vera in Act II, before they have answered her, "I've been in the cellar.":

> Vera: Then you've seen what happens when it isn't held. All of them would do that. Dan would do that. Don't touch the wheel.

Once. It is not an answer to her offer; the offer's three answers stay.

PUT_IT_BACK is the script's. Every other line is new: WEDGE_DRIVEN, WEDGE_OUT, WEDGE_LIES, WEDGE_BACK, THIRD_WAY, R_CELLAR and
