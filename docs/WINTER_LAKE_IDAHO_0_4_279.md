# Indian Lake, in Idaho: Winter Lake (0.4.279)

The owner: "Consider actual regional landscaping and architecture." Winter Lake is the same Idaho mountain lake as Movie Night and
Costume Night (0.4.278), frozen. Its banks were a flat snowfield with a row of identical spruce round three sides. This pass gives
them the shape and the forest of a mountain lake in winter. Every place the story uses is where it was. New builds only.

## The banks (`WinterLake`)

- **They rise.** The near bank, where the boys keep their fire, stays level. The side banks and the far end climb steeply into the
  forest, to six or seven blocks at the scene's edge (`bank`).
- **They are not a rectangle.** The far corners are rounded, and the foot of the slope wanders a block or two either way along each
  side.
- **Snow and granite.** The slopes are drifted snow over dirt. Where they are too steep for drift, grey granite shows through.
- **Granite at the edge.** Boulders sit half under the drift along the side banks and the far bank, at the ice. There are none on
  the near bank, where the boys go out onto the ice.

## The woods (`WinterLake`, `IdahoShore`)

- **The trees** are lodgepole pine, Douglas fir and subalpine fir, as at the other Indian Lake scenes, with snow on their boughs.
- **The aspens stand bare.** Down by the ice, the aspens are pale trunks with a few grey twigs at the top (`IdahoShore.bareAspen`).
- **Snow lies on everything:** needles, boulders and drifts alike (`IdahoShore.snowCap`), never above the scene's ceiling.
- **The Douglas firs have needles all the way up.** Every layer of the crown has at least a ring of needles, and the top two cells
  are needles, not trunk. This also changes the firs at Movie Night and Costume Night (new builds only).

## Untouched

- the shanty and its way on;

## The architecture view

The literary outdoor view cut every scene at seven blocks. That sliced the Idaho banks' trees off at the trunk, at Movie Night and
Costume Night as well as here. Those three scenes are now cut at seventeen blocks, so the whole forest shows.
