# Indian Lake: the cave and Shallows — 0.4.12

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.251 or later · matching 0.4.12 client and server

## The cave of the preserved dead

A waterline causeway leads into a natural rock chamber. Twenty-four fresh bodies sit in six rows, all facing the lake. Distinct coats, work clothes and windbreakers place them across different years. The aisle is physically walkable; an explorer's field book near the mouth teaches quiet movement. Behind the last pew is a real, fixed canoe.

Crouched footsteps are silent. Ordinary native footsteps, swimming, landing, eating, drinking, doors and other player-caused vibrations add voices to an original wordless hymn. Projectiles belong to their shooter. The congregation and choir cannot amplify themselves. Eight distinct positional mono stems form the full hymn; the front eight bodies rise at four voices, the middle eight at six, and the last eight at eight. The transition animates gradually. Fighting the people darkens that visit and prevents its resolution; leaving and returning makes it retryable.

Examine the canoe at the back, then walk out through the real return door to record the congregation in the Witness account. Beginning a visit alone is not a resolution. Opening Drowned Town's church roof applies its permanent consequence on the next unoccupied cave arrival: the pews remain empty and their occupants stand on the shore. Another player's arrival cannot reset the current hymn or move bodies somebody is crossing between. Examining the canoe and returning after this change completes the shared final beat; later explorers can still examine its aftermath for their own account.

The canoe has a saved, owner-specific retrieval hook for the future Phone in the Canoe encounter. It returns the original item with every recorded component intact, once. A full inventory leaves it in the canoe, and another explorer cannot take it. There is no invented recording or automatically awarded phone: the filming encounter is not part of this update. A real stored phone can make the cave available again to its owner after its final beat.

## The Shallows

This shore recollection is dealt only after this particular player has been genuinely hunted in Drowned Town. A command-placed door obeys the same rule. Three boys stand on the bank; the familiar steeple lies beyond the water. Stacey uses the Witch's articulated model with a separate recollection material and passive poses.

Empty both hands and right-click her. Carry her to the water's edge, face the open lake, briefly hold use (at least 0.6 seconds), and release. The body actually travels under native gravity; the recorded participation occurs only when she reaches the water. There is no timer that assumes the throw landed. The school essays in the player's inventory gain the roster page immediately. Other copies update when opened by a participant. Their username and personal Witness passage are saved once.

Entering, holding her, an interrupted carry, or another player's completion do not count. Leaving is allowed and keeps an unfinished scene retryable. Temporary grips are owner-bound, cleaned on leaving and refused when stale. Dropping the grip releases the carry without creating a collectible item. Each explorer has their own actor and exhaustion flag, so one participant cannot finish the vignette for everyone else. Attacking its people darkens that visit; it does not replace the throwing beat.

## Door traces and existing saves

The cave door leaks a low voice beneath water; Shallows leaks water against reeds. Both leave animated water and dripping at the closed threshold. These are private hints tied to the player's door deal. Existing rare impossible environments, recent-visit suppression, companions and rest pacing retain their current rules.

Builder version 15 adds slots 28 and 29 when upgrading a version-14 labyrinth. It does not rebuild the school, refill its finite inventories, reset the church, erase drops, or move existing rooms. Previous supported migrations retain their rules. Network protocol 14 includes the new leak kinds and actor states.

## Testing controls

The House must exist and the labyrinth must finish carving first.

- `/oldesthouse door preserved_cave`
- `/oldesthouse vignette preserved_cave status`
- `/oldesthouse vignette preserved_cave reset` — resets the local visit; the church's permanent consequence stays.
- `/oldesthouse door shallows`
- `/oldesthouse vignette shallows status`
- `/oldesthouse vignette shallows unlock` — **debug only**, records your hunt without playing it.
- `/oldesthouse vignette shallows reset` — resets only your participation; peers stay unchanged.
