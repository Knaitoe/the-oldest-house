# Static House architecture

The overworld House is intentionally conventional. Its exterior is the fixed measuring reference against which the impossible interior will later be judged.

## Prototype footprint

- 15 blocks wide
- 19 blocks deep
- masonry foundation
- white plaster/terracotta walls with dark timber corners
- steep dark-oak gable roof
- covered front porch
- brick fireplace and chimney
- small attic windows

The rear central hall intentionally ends at an ordinary blank exterior wall. That exact wall is reserved for the first impossible-door event. Until then, the entire domestic floor plan fits inside the static exterior.

## Domestic purpose

The House is meant to be attractive enough to use:

- crafting/furnace/smoker work area
- storage barrels/chests
- bed
- study/bookshelves/lectern
- fireplace
- dining area
- hanging lantern lighting

Player ownership should precede horror. The impossible area comes later.
