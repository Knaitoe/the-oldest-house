# The list, part 9a: what is kept (0.4.731)

The first of what *The list* leaves with its readers (`docs/THE_LIST_PLAN.md`, part 9). No protocol, layout or Witness change; the
Witness line and the together line were already written in 0.4.721.

## The keepsakes

Given once, when a reader's own account resolves the story, through the same path as every other room's keepsake.

- **The one who went in: a strip of the feed sack.** "Torn from the sack at an eyehole, stiff where the blood dried. Out in your world,
  once a day, what should kill you only puts you down." Out in their own world, once a House day, a blow that would kill them leaves
  half a heart instead; they are slowed hard for five seconds and told "You go down. You get up." The sack and the hay hook still never
  leave the town; the strip is not the sack.
- **Everyone else who was in town: a page from Amber's notebook.** "One of her rules, in her hand: they can hear you. Out in your
  world, you hear them first." Out in their own world, when something that hunts turns on them, they hear its heart, for them alone, at
  most once every ten seconds.
- **Spectators** keep nothing, as before.

## The account afterwards

The Witness account adds, from what the reader's own keepsake record holds: "Afterwards, once, out in my world, something should have
killed me and only put me down. I got up." (or how many times), and "Afterwards I heard a heart before I saw what wanted me, once." (or
how many times).

## Single and multiplayer

- **Alone**, the reader is the one who went in and keeps the strip.
- **With others**, the one who went in keeps the strip; each reader on Amber's side keeps their own page; each keepsake works only for
  its owner and counts only for them.
