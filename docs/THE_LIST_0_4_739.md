# The list, stand-ins 5: the five walk to the field house (0.4.739)

The last of the stand-ins `docs/THE_LIST_PLAN.md` lists. No protocol, layout or Witness change.

Part 6c (`docs/THE_LIST_0_4_725.md`) had the five already standing in the field house's locker room when night three's hunt began.
Now they come to it.

- When night three's hunt begins the five who are left are on Main Street's sidewalk east of the field house's street door, which stands
  open. One after another, a second apart, they go in at the street door, round the corner inside, through the gap in the bench, to where
  each stands. When the last of them is in, the street door is shut and bolted behind them, as the back door already is.
- One the slasher startles on the way is the hunt's from then on, wherever they are. One held up on the way (by a wall, a bench end, or
  another of them) with no progress for four seconds is let past to their next step; any still out after forty-five seconds is let in
  where they belong.
- Everyone in town sees them go in. A reader may follow them in while the door is open, or be on the wrong side of it once it shuts.

Checked in the field house case: at the start of night three the five are on their way in, all outside on the sidewalk, and the street
door is open and not bolted; they are then all in their places, calm, with every door bolted. Run with prints at each step, all five
walk in on their own within ten seconds, with none let past.
