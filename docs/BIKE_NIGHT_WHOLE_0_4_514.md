# Bike night 31: the whole night, once through (0.4.514)

Every part of Bike night has its own cases. Until now nothing had gone from the park to the lane in one night, and the
docs said so each time: "Nobody has played the night from the park to the lane."

This is one case that does, for two readers. It adds no behaviour. It found one thing.

## The night it plays

1. **The park.** Two readers come in. One takes the Hopper and the other the Wasp. Gus is there.
2. **The three** come, Tully says go, and the cemetery gate stands open. Each reader has who rode with them.
3. **The town.** The Wasp stands on Main Street until one of the three has it: its rider is caught, and not out,
   because a friend is still riding. The Hopper keeps away on Church Lane. Each has the street they were on.
4. **The pegs.** The Hopper comes back, and a hand on it puts the caught reader on its pegs.
5. **The cemetery.** Up to the top of the hill. The three fall back and are gone, Tully with the cap on.
6. **The Hollow**, two up, by the plain way, over every gap, and neither goes down.
7. Behind them the three's bikes lie at the front gate; ahead, the cap is on the back gate's post.
8. **Out through the back gate**: one riding, one on the pegs. Through, and not finished until they leave.
9. **Gus** comes off the Hollow after them and takes his cap down.
10. **The lane's door**, each for themselves: each has finished it, once, with the Witness and their own account, and
    is not dealt it again. One rode out; the other came out on the pegs.
11. **The accounts**, each in the order it happened: the bike, Tully saying go, who rode with them, the street, who
    carried whom, the three falling back, the way through the Hollow, how they came out, and the door in the wall.

## What it found

**A tap does not carry two.** With somebody on the pegs a push and a hop get 85% through (`NightBike.CARRYING`). The
quick tap that takes one rider over two blocks of nothing does not take two: the first time this ran, the Hopper came
down a hair under the next grave's edge at speed and threw them both.

Wound up, it does. Holding the hop from two and a half blocks short of each gap of two, and from four short of the gap
of three, the Hopper carries both over the whole plain way without going down once. So carrying a friend through the
Hollow is possible and is harder, which is what the pegs were for: "That makes losing a bike in the chase a co-op
rescue rather than the end."

Nothing in the game says so. Somebody carrying a friend who taps as they did alone will be thrown at the first gap,
and will have to find out. Left with the owner: whether Gus, or the first fall, should say something.
