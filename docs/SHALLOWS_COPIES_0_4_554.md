# Six to a bank: the Shallows by copy (0.4.554)

The owner, 13 November: "All multiplayer vignettes should be hooked through that system. If full".

Since 0.4.157 the Shallows has been one bank for everyone on it (`docs/SHALLOWS_TOGETHER_0_4_157.md`). A reader who comes while another's
recollection is under way joins it: the first to join takes her feet in Lonnie's place, and anyone after that stands on the bank with Bud, Hank
and Clay. A reader who comes when there is none to join begins one of their own, on another of the bank's three carry places. A fourth
recollection at once had no carry place of its own, and would have been laid where the first one lay. This release gives the bank copies and
a number of places.

## Full

| Story | Places | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The Shallows | 6 | two hands to each of the bank's three carry places: her wrists and her feet |

A bank's places are held by the readers on it now. A reader who walks off the bank leaves the recollection they were part of, as they always
have, and keeps no place.

- A bank is full to a reader coming to it when its six places are held, or when all three of its carry places are taken and none of its
  recollections can be joined (`Shallows.room`): one is joinable while it is under way and not yet thrown, its reader is on the bank, and
  she is not yet in the air.
- The seventh, or anyone a bank turns away, goes to the next bank, built whole a million blocks west, with three boys of its own. Their
  door waits while it is made ready; it is made ready ahead when a bank is down to its last place.
- Every record keeps the bank it stands on (`Copy0554`). A recollection's carry place is chosen among that bank's own; a reader joins only a
  recollection on their own bank; the boys who speak and who go home are that bank's (kept as `boy0@1`, `boy1@1`, ... for copy 1, the
  original's as they always were); and a reader who comes to another bank leaves the recollection they were part of on the first.
- The lake's night cue (`LakeLandscape`, scene mode 7) reaches a reader on a copy of a lake as it reaches one on the original: the
  phone canoe's copies have it too now.
- The accounts already count company within one copy (0.4.547), so a reader's line says who was on their own bank.

## The case

`IndianLakeLinkedTests.aSeventhReaderIsGivenABankOfTheirOwn`: six on the bank, one at her wrists, one at her feet and four with the boys,
are full to a seventh and never to one of the six. The seventh's door waits while a second bank is made ready; one of the six goes back to
theirs. The second bank stands a million blocks west; the seventh goes to it by its own door and begins a recollection of their own there, on
its first carry place though the first bank's is taken, with a boy at her feet and three boys on the bank of its own. Neither recollection
is sent to the other's reader, and the first bank's is as it was. A bank whose three carry places are taken with nobody to join has no room
on it, and another bank does; a carry place freed is room again.

## Decided here (any of it can move)

- Six places: two hands to each of the bank's three carry places.
- A bank with every carry place taken and nobody to join is full whatever its count.
- A reader who walks off the bank keeps no place, since they leave the recollection when they do.
