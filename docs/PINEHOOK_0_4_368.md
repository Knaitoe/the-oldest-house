# Last Weekend at Pinehook, slice 5 (0.4.368)

The proposal's last branches (`docs/PINEHOOK_PROPOSAL.md`). Slice 4 (`docs/PINEHOOK_0_4_367.md`) finished the weekend; reading the proposal against it left six things out. This slice adds them.

- **Witness:** unchanged, 81 / 61 / two kinds / three endings.
- **Protocol:** 161. **Layout:** 121. No new textures or models.

## Len keeps his safe shut

> *"Len tries to keep the safe shut if the group has not confronted him."*

- **In the crisis:** if Len is shut in and nobody has faced him with the safe, he says nobody is opening anything down there.
- **At the safe:** a reader who tries the key or the hook on it, or reaches into it, finds Len standing in the way. Nothing happens to the safe.
- **Facing him:** in the crisis, a reader inside who has seen Nina go or faced her, heard Jude name her, read the envelope or heard Casey's confession can say *"She's coming for what's in that safe. Step away from it."* Len gives up the safe and says where the key is.
- **Already faced:** asking him about the safe in the night, the question that gets the key, counts too.

## Jude won't help Casey

> *"Jude can identify an attacker, refuse to help Casey, or ask a player to stay while the others leave."*

When Casey, who never owned up, starts down to the rowboat, he asks Jude for a hand. Two seconds later Jude says no. Anyone within 32 blocks of Jude hears it.

## The boat can take Nina away

> *"It can evacuate Jude, move Nina, or let Casey flee."*

- **When:** the boat is ready and moored, and Nina has stopped. That means she is held, has given up her knife, has given up, or Len's hold on her is gone.
- **What:** a reader can tell her *"The boat's ready. Take it, and go."* If she is being held, the readers let go of her.
- **Then:** she walks down to the boat from wherever she is and rows out onto the lake. The boat goes with her, so it can't also take Jude across.
- **First light:** waits until she has gone.
- **Family:** with Len's envelope still private, this is Len's account.

## Or she can be stopped for good

> *"The party restrains or kills Nina while allowing Len to retain the evidence."*

- **Who:** a reader carrying the boat hook, in front of Nina while she has the knife out, at the cleat, inside or hunting the cabin.
- **What:** that reader can swing it. She goes down and doesn't get up, and stays down through first light.
- **Records:** the one who swung it records it, and anyone who saw it records that too.
- **Family:** unless the envelope is out or ash, this is Len's account. The morning has Nina on the dock where she fell, and Len saying, more than once, that nobody would blame us.

## Len's account, undone at first light

> *"A participant who knows more can still expose Len afterward, changing the coda."*

- **Before:** in 0.4.367, telling Len *"That's not all that happened"* only went in that reader's own account.
- **Now:** at first light, before anyone's account is written, it changes the weekend for everyone. The reader tells everyone what was in the envelope, Casey says he is driving to the store to tell someone everything (Jude says it if Casey has gone), and the family becomes **both exposed**.
- **Records:** every account starts its morning with Len beginning to tell it his way. The reader who said it records that it was them, and anyone near records hearing it.

## Each part pays off

> *"The roles supply expectations. The player's acts supply the character arc."*

Each reader's morning ends with a line weighing the part Casey gave them against what they did:

- **The Athlete:**
  - with a brace across a door: carried a length of timber and never chased anybody;
  - having held Nina: got hold of somebody and didn't let go;
  - having guarded the cleat: stood still on a dock.
- **The Skeptic:** got Casey to stop calling it a bit. Or, having challenged the mask story or Len's account, was the one saying in the morning that it had happened.
- **The Charmer:** asked Nina what Len had said to her, and the party was over after that.
- **The Wildcard:** whichever small thing they found made the others listen: the tape, the strap, the buckle or the wet prints.
- **The Horror Fan:** at first light, can tell Casey *"I had it all worked out. I had the wrong person."* The account says so. Without it, nothing went the way these go.
- **The Responsible One:** kept Jude alive (dressed or sent across). If they also did one of these, the account says both are theirs:
  - opened the slip for her;
  - braced Len in;
  - gave him back his envelope;
  - swung the hook;
  - sent Nina away;
  - went along with the mask story.

A reader with no part gets no payoff line.

## Multiplayer

- **Shared:** Len's guard on the safe, Jude's refusal, Nina's fate, the boat and the family change at first light.
- **Private:** each reader's record keeps who swung the hook, who sent her away, who faced Len and who told everyone. Two readers in one family get different accounts.
- **One reader alone** can do all of it. The boat needs nobody else when Nina has given up or her leverage is gone. When she has to be held first, that still takes two, or Casey once he has turned.
