# A Place for You: somebody at the door (0.4.686)

The second slice of the plan (`docs/A_PLACE_FOR_YOU_PLAN.md`), the proposal's N8 and N9: Theo at the door, the bargain, and the
private questions before the threshold. The first slice (`docs/A_PLACE_FOR_YOU_0_4_257.md`) ended the opening of the house as soon
as the hall chair held the front door and the reader's picture was down. Now somebody knocks first, and the house makes its offer
while the door stands open. Mara and Alex still decide for themselves at the end, but only once the voice has stopped.

Code: `PlaceForYouDoor` (new), hooks in `PlaceForYou`, the pictures and the reader's chair in `PlaceForYouSite`, the account in
`PlaceForYouTexts`, and the filled picture in `tools/draw_place_for_you_art.py`.

## What happens

- **The knock.** Once Kit is out through the gate (or twelve seconds after Kit set off) and the reader is in the house with no
  conversation open, the front door swings shut unless the hall chair is holding it. A moment later there are three knocks. Mara
  says above her head that nobody knocks there.
- **The voice.** A hand on the door after the knock opens "Through the door": a man's voice, asking about the spare key. The
  reader can ask who he is, what's for supper and where he has been, open the door, or leave it shut. Opened, there is nobody on
  the step, and a moment later the voice comes from by the gate. If the door was already open when he knocked, he knocks on the
  frame, and the voice comes the same way.
- **The bargain.** While the door stands open, the house offers its old evening a stage at a time, each where the reader can see
  it or come to it (ten seconds a stage):
  1. Ren says "Mara. The pictures." The gap in the household's row fills with a man in a work shirt, as the reader looks at it,
     or later while they are out of the sitting room.
  2. The reader's chair goes from the table while they are out of the kitchen. The next time they are in the kitchen, they see
     it.
  3. The first of the household the reader sat with (never Kit) asks if they have met, and a moment later takes it back.
  Once the chair has gone, Ren says aloud what letting him in would cost, as soon as the reader is near enough and in sight.
- **Shut.** With the door shut, the house lets things go back. Each one goes back while the reader is not looking: the pictures
  are emptied and the chair returned. While the door stays shut, the voice knocks again every 30 seconds, three times at most.
- **The last word.** With the hall chair holding the door and the reader's picture down, the voice says her name once more, and
  stops. Only then will Mara and Alex decide (E1), as before.
- **Before the threshold.** Kit, still on the way out, can be asked what help they want. While the knocking goes on, Mara can be
  asked whether she wants them to stay or wants them unable to leave, and whether it is him. Alex and Ren can be asked whether
  they go together, and what happens if she lets him in. Anyone else spoken to says above their head that they won't answer it.
- **The road.** "Keep walking" now works from the open house too (E4). It is recorded as leaving them to it, not as a refusal.
  If the voice was still asking, the account says so.

Nothing here can hurt, nothing has a clock that ends the evening, and every stage that changes the room waits for the reader to
be where it can be seen or come to.

## With more than one reader

Each reader's evening is a copy of its own (as in slice 1), so the knock, the voice, the bargain and the questions are theirs
alone. Nothing new is shared.
