# The list, part 9b: the town afterwards (0.4.733)

More of what *The list* leaves (`docs/THE_LIST_PLAN.md`, part 9). No protocol, layout or Witness change.

A reader who has finished the four nights (their account read) can be dealt Lamesa again, twice at most, as every finished scene on the
aftermath list can (`Aftermath`). They come in through their own back door and find the town at noon, the clock stopped, nothing begun
and nothing to earn, and one line written from how their nights ended:

- **Names crossed off** (some or all): "Noon. The flyers are still up on the poles, gone pale in the sun. Nobody is out walking."
- **No names crossed off**: "Noon. Everybody on the list is somewhere in town, doing nothing much. None of them looks at you twice."
- **Everyone got away**, or **got them out**: "Noon. Five houses in town have their curtains open on empty rooms."
- **The bus**: "Noon. The bench outside the feed store is empty. The schedule on the post says the bus comes once a day, at first light."
- **Stopped by Stace**: "Noon. Somebody has leaned a shovel against the foot of the water tower and left it there."
- **In town at the end**: "Noon in Lamesa. It looks like any town."

The line comes once a visit and the visit counts as one return.

## Single and multiplayer

- **Alone**, a finished reader gets the noon town.
- **With others**, only while every reader in the town has finished it; a reader who has not had a night there keeps the town as it is
  for them, and no return is counted for anyone.

New case `theTownAfter` (a finished reader alone, then with a reader who has not begun).
